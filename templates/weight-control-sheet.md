# Weight-Control Sheet (Fiche de Contrôle des Poids)

Count the batch against the order, weigh a sample of cooled pieces and decide what to do with the batch. Method, salt limits and the decision table are in lesson [15.4](../lessons/module-15/lesson-04.md).

## Sample weights (g)

At least 5 cooled pieces (10 for a large batch), taken from different places: first and last trays, each deck, front and back.
