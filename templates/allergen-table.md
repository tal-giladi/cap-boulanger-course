# Allergen Table (Tableau des Allergènes)

The written grid of products × the 14 regulated allergens, kept in the shop and shown to any customer who asks. Fill it from the technical sheets and from the label or supplier sheet of every bought ingredient, including egg wash, chocolate, raisins, improvers, malt and premixes. Every cell is ticked or left empty on purpose, never "?". The 14 allergens and where they hide are in lesson [17.6](../lessons/module-17/lesson-06.md).
