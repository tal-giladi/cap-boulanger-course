# EP1 Error List (Liste d'Erreurs)

One line for every point lost in an EP1-style round or mock paper. The list names the habits behind lost points, not just the wrong answers; rework the lines before your next round. Method in lesson [21.1](../lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md); record your round scores in the [readiness checklist](readiness-checklist.md).

Causes: **knowledge** (did not know it), **verb** (misread citer, définir, expliquer, justifier…), **working** (no formula or working shown), **unit** (missing or wrong unit), **document figure** (no figure taken from the document), **time** (not reached or rushed).
