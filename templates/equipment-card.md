# Equipment Card (Fiche Matériel et Fiche de Poste)

One card per machine or appliance: mixer, divider, sheeter, proofing cabinet, cold room, oven. Keep it beside the machine. Every safety line names the page of the maker's manual it comes from; a line with no page is your opinion: check it or delete it (lesson [13.2](../lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md)).
