# Cold-Unit Temperature Log (Relevé de Températures des Enceintes Froides)

One sheet per cold unit (fridge, cold room, retarder, display cabinet, freezer), read every day at a fixed time, ideally before work starts. Keep the sheets at least 12 months. Limits and what to do after a break are in lesson [17.8](../lessons/module-17/lesson-08.md). For dough temperatures use the [temperature log](temperature-log.md).
