# Templates

Printable or copyable working documents used across the course. Copy one into your notebook or a file and fill it in; lessons tell you when.

- [Production sheet (fiche technique)](production-sheet.md): formula in grams and baker's percentages, process and targets.
- [Organigramme](organigramme.md): the timed work plan for one or several products.
- [Bake log](bake-log.md): what you did, what you measured, how it turned out.
- [Temperature log](temperature-log.md): the base-temperature calculation and the temperatures you measured.
- [Delivery check](delivery-check.md): accept, reject or report a delivery.
- [Quality rubric](quality-rubric.md): score a product's exterior, interior and taste.
- [Non-conformity report](non-conformity-report.md): report a problem with goods, equipment or production.
- [Readiness checklist](readiness-checklist.md): what you can do, what you still need before the exam, with evidence.
- [Equipment card (fiche de poste)](equipment-card.md): one machine's plate data, settings, safe use, cleaning and checks.
- [Maintenance log](maintenance-log.md): dated checks of scales, thermometers and guards, and what you did when one failed.
- [Weight-control sheet](weight-control-sheet.md): count a batch, weigh a sample, check salt and decide.
- [Tasting sheet](tasting-sheet.md): blind tasting with intensity scores and liking scored separately.
- [Cold-unit temperature log](cold-unit-log.md): daily readings of fridges, cold rooms and freezers, with actions.
- [Cleaning plan](cleaning-plan.md): what is cleaned, with what, how often, by whom and who checks.
- [Allergen table](allergen-table.md): products × the 14 allergens, with possible traces.
- [Waste log](waste-log.md): what is thrown away or unsold, why, where it went and what it cost.
- [Environmental audit](environmental-audit.md): a week of energy, water, waste and packaging figures and the action plan.
- [Pitch card (fiche argumentaire)](pitch-card.md): the seven blocks the sales staff need for one product.
- [Hand-over brief](hand-over-brief.md): what to tell the next baker or the sales staff.
- [CV and cover letter](cv-and-cover-letter.md): a one-page French CV and a vous / moi / nous letter.
- [Payslip check](payslip-check.md): your hours notebook, weekly overtime and a line-by-line payslip comparison.
- [Rights card](rights-card.md): the work rules you need, each with its source and the date you checked it.
- [EP1 error list](ep1-error-list.md): every point lost, its cause and the habit you will change.
