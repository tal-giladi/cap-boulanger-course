# Payslip Check (Carnet d'Heures et Contrôle de la Fiche de Paie)

Keep your own hours every day, then compare them with the payslip. Rates, thresholds, the SMIC and the convention's grid change: take today's figures from lesson [20.4](../lessons/module-20/lesson-04.md) and your [rights card](rights-card.md), and write the date you checked them.
