# Cleaning and Disinfection Plan (Plan de Nettoyage et de Désinfection)

The written plan for every zone and item: what, with what, how, how often, who does it and who checks. Post it where the cleaning is done. The protocol and Sinner's circle (temperature, mechanical action, concentration, contact time) are in lesson [17.4](../lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md).
