# Hand-Over Brief (Passation de Consignes)

Notes for a brief you then say aloud, following the production order, not your memory. To the next baker: under about 45 seconds (lesson [19.1](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md)). To the sales staff: about two minutes (lesson [15.5](../lessons/module-15/lesson-05.md)). Ask the listener to repeat back the times and places.
