# Quality Rubric

Score every bake the same way so you can compare them. Cut bread only after it has fully cooled. The Academy cannot grade photos: compare your product honestly with these descriptions. This self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.

## Bread (out of 20)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weight within ±3% of target | outside ±5% | within ±5% | within ±3% | |
| Shape regular and as ordered | misshapen | slightly uneven | regular, all pieces alike | |
| Volume | flat or dense | slightly low | full, light in the hand | |
| Scoring opened (grigne) | not opened | partly opened | open, regular, with an ear | |
| Crust colour | pale or burnt | uneven | even golden-brown | |
| Crust texture | soft or leathery | thick or slightly soft | crisp, thin, crackles | |
| Crumb structure for the product | dense or tunnelled | uneven | as expected (open for tradition, fine and regular for pain de mie) | |
| Crumb colour and texture | grey, gummy or dry | slightly dull or tight | creamy, moist, elastic | |
| Aroma and taste | flat, sour or yeasty | correct but simple | rich, balanced, correctly salted | |
| Bottom and sides | burnt or raw | slightly pale or dark | baked through, even | |
