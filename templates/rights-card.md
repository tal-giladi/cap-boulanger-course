# Rights Card (Mes Droits au Travail)

One page with the rules you need on the job. Each rule has its figure, its source and the date you checked it: figures change (the SMIC is revalued, the convention's grid is renegotiated), so check them again before you rely on them. The rules and where to find them are in lesson [20.4](../lessons/module-20/lesson-04.md).
