# Waste Log (Suivi des Pertes et des Invendus)

Weigh and record everything you throw away or do not sell, every day, with its reason and where it went. A week of entries shows where the waste comes from; the plan then attacks that source first, following the food-waste hierarchy of lesson [18.2](../lessons/module-18/lesson-02.md): prevent, use for people, animal feed, compost or methanisation through the biowaste collection, and residual waste only for what is left.
