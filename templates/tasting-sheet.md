# Tasting Sheet (Fiche d'Analyse Sensorielle)

Score what you sense, how strongly, and only then whether you like it. Method, descriptors and tests are in lesson [15.3](../lessons/module-15/lesson-03.md).

Tasters: no coffee, smoking or toothpaste for 30 minutes before; no talking; water and about a minute between samples. Taste in order: crumb (look, smell, press), crust (smell, press, listen), then both together.
