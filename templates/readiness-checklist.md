# Readiness Checklist

Tick only what you can do now, without notes, at the standard described. Everything unticked is what you still need before the exam. Physical skills need practice on professional equipment as well as at home.

## EP1 knowledge

- [ ] I can explain the wheat-flour-bread chain and the French flour types.
- [ ] I can explain the role of water, salt, yeast, levain, sugar, fat, milk and eggs.
- [ ] I can explain each stage of bread-making and of viennoiserie, and the main defects and their causes.
- [ ] I can apply hygiene rules, HACCP principles, the cold chain and allergen rules to a scenario.
- [ ] I can check a delivery and write a non-conformity report.
- [ ] I can answer applied-management questions: contracts, pay, costs, VAT, stock.
- [ ] I have done a full 2-hour EP1 mock paper in time.

## EP2 written phase

- [ ] I can complete a technical sheet from an order in 15 minutes with no calculation error.
- [ ] I can draw an organigramme for four products in 15 minutes.

## EP2 practical

- [ ] I can hit a target dough temperature within ±1 °C.
- [ ] I can divide to weight within ±3%, quickly.
- [ ] I can shape baguettes, an épi, two shaped pieces and three roll shapes.
- [ ] I can make one of: pain complet, pain viennois, pain de mie, pain de campagne.
- [ ] I can laminate, cut and shape 12 croissants, 12 pains au chocolat and 12 pains aux raisins.
- [ ] I can make pain au lait or pain brioché as braids, navettes or animals.
- [ ] I have worked on a spiral mixer, a deck oven and a sheeter.
- [ ] I have done a full production day in about 6 h 30 and cleaned my station.

## Administrative

- [ ] I know my académie's registration dates and have registered.
- [ ] I know which general subjects I must sit or am exempt from.
- [ ] I have any workplace-training certificates my académie asks for.
- [ ] I have my professional clothing and protective equipment for the practical.
