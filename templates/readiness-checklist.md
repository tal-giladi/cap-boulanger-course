# Readiness Checklist

Tick only what you can do now, without notes, at the standard described, and **tick only with evidence**: write in the Evidence column the date and the log, sheet or score that proves it (bake log, temperature log, quality rubric, mock-paper score). A line you cannot back with evidence stays unticked. Everything unticked is what you still need before the exam. Physical skills need practice on professional equipment as well as at home. Durations, marks and the list of products are on [The CAP Boulanger Exam](../references/cap-exam.md).
