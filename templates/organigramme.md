# Organigramme (Work Schedule)

The organigramme lays every product's stages on one timeline so that the mixer, the bench and the oven are never needed by two products at once. In the EP2 written phase you complete one for the whole order.

Use short codes: W = weigh, M = mix, P = pointage, D = divide, R = détente, S = shape, A = apprêt, B = bake, C = clean.

The **You (hands)** column shows what your hands are doing; a dough can ferment while you work on another product, but you can only do one hands task at a time. The **Proofing place** and **Cold space** columns (optional) show which product is in the proofing cabinet and in the fridge or retarder in each slot, with the setting. Lessons [14.1](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md) and [14.3](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md) build a full example.

## Checks

- [ ] No two products need the oven at the same time unless it can take both (same temperature, enough space).
- [ ] The longest process starts first.
- [ ] Laminated dough rests in the fridge between turns while bread is in pointage.
- [ ] The hands column never has two blocks at once: small tasks (fold, egg wash, unload) may slip a few minutes, blocks (mixing, dividing, shaping, turns) may not overlap.
- [ ] One setting for everything in the proofing cabinet at the same time (laminated dough below about 27 °C).
- [ ] Oven heating and cooling times measured on your oven and written as lines in the plan.
- [ ] Crème pâtissière: the time it leaves the heat (63 °C) and the time it reaches 10 °C are on the plan, no more than 2 h apart.
- [ ] Cleaning and tidying are in the plan, including at the end.
- [ ] Finish time is before the deadline with 15 minutes spare.
