# Environmental Audit (Audit Environnemental)

One week of a bakery or kitchen turned into figures, then into an action plan someone can follow. Methods and figures are in lessons [18.1](../lessons/module-18/lesson-01.md) (energy and water), [18.2](../lessons/module-18/lesson-02.md) (waste) and [18.3](../lessons/module-18/lesson-03.md) (packaging and cleaning products).
