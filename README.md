# CAP Boulanger Preparation

A free, self-paced course that takes a complete beginner from first principles to exam-style practice for the French CAP Boulanger (bakery worker) qualification: ingredients, dough science, fermentation, bread and viennoiserie production, hygiene, planning and quality, with something to bake, calculate or plan at home in every lesson.

This free course prepares you for the CAP Boulanger. It is not the CAP itself and does not award the French national diploma. The CAP is awarded by the French Ministry of Education after the official examination; see [The CAP Boulanger Exam](references/cap-exam.md) (curriculum version RNCP42115, checked 2026-10-08).

## Who it is for

Anyone who cooks at home, has a domestic oven, knows no French bakery terms, and wants to work as a boulanger or sit the CAP Boulanger as an individual candidate.

## What this course covers

- The professional parts of the CAP: EP1 (technology, applied sciences, applied management) and EP2 (production).
- 22 modules, 124 lessons, each with a short multiple-choice quiz and a practical task at home.
- A practical project at the end of every module, ending in a full production day rehearsal.
- French professional terms taught as French → English, collected in the [glossary](glossary.md).

## What it does not cover: additional preparation required

The general subjects of the CAP are outside this course: French, history-geography and civic education; mathematics and physics-chemistry; a foreign language; physical education; and prevention-health-environment (PSE, covered only partly). Unless you are exempt, you must prepare for them separately. The [exam page](references/cap-exam.md) explains the exemption rules as the official sources give them.

## Learning by doing

Watching someone make bread does not make you able to make bread. Every lesson ends with something you do at home, and you judge your result against a rubric. The Academy cannot grade photos of your bakes, and self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam. Reaching exam standard also needs practice on professional equipment (spiral mixer, deck oven, sheeter), for example during workplace training.

> [!WARNING]
> This course involves hot ovens and steam, hot surfaces, sharp blades, electric mixers and common food allergens (wheat/gluten, milk, eggs, soy, sesame, nuts). Follow the safety notes in each lesson.
