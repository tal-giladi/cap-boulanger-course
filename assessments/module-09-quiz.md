# Module 9 Quiz: Pain de Tradition Française

This quiz checks whether you can run a tradition batch from the documents a bakery actually uses: checking a mill's specification sheet and a laboratory report against the bread decree, refusing a freezing shortcut with the right article, scaling a TR-01 order that must also provide tomorrow's tradition pâte fermentée, calculating the water with the pâte fermentée as fourth factor, adjusting pointage to a dough that left the mixer off target, diagnosing a white, bland crumb and an over-fermented summer batch, checking salt per 100 g with a measured loss, and writing a true description for the sales staff. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
