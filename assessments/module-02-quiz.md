# Module 2 Quiz: Ingredients

This quiz checks whether you can use module 2 in realistic bakery situations: reading flour bags and miller's certificates, choosing a flour for a product, adjusting water, salt and yeast with real numbers, managing a levain within the law, using enrichments and correcting products correctly, storing fillings at the right temperature and reading nutrition labels. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
