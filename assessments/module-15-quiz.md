# Module 15 Quiz: Product Quality

This quiz checks whether you can judge finished products the way a bakery does, from the documents it actually uses: diagnosing a pale, flat batch from its record, deciding what to do with an under-baked pain de mie after a cut test, choosing the right sensory test for a question, calculating salt per 100 g from a weight-control sheet, applying the prepackage rules to a lot of bagged brioche, correcting a display plan, improving flavour from a tasting sheet, reacting to a failed shop scale, writing a factual non-conformity note and comparing a sodium label with your own bread at home in Israel. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
