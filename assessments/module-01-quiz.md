# Module 1 Quiz: Introduction to Professional Baking

This quiz checks whether you can use module 1 in realistic bakery situations: organising work and hygiene around the marche en avant, weighing and correcting instrument readings, calculating batches with baker's percentages, reading orders and technical sheets, planning backwards from a deadline and knowing where exam rules come from. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
