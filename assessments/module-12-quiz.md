# Module 12 Quiz: Pain au Chocolat, Pain aux Raisins and Brioche Doughs

This quiz checks whether you can run the viennoiserie bench from the documents a bakery actually uses: calculating the flour for a mixed order of croissants and pains au chocolat from one lamination, reading a crème pâtissière cooling record, diagnosing pale pains aux raisins from a bake log, finding the liquid temperature from a printed TB, writing the allergen line for a shop label, choosing a safe thawing schedule for frozen raw croissants, calculating a braid and navette order, tracing leaking butter to a warm proof, giving a customer the salt per 100 g of a brioche and deciding what to do with an out-of-date freezer bag. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
