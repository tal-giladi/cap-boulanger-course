# Module 13 Quiz: Professional Equipment

This quiz checks whether you can run and look after a bakery's equipment from the documents it produces: splitting a production order into mixer batches from the mixer's technical sheet, weighing a divider load, deciding what to do with a sheeter whose guard was bypassed, reading a proofing-cabinet temperature log and a retarder-proofer record, costing an oven's empty running from an energy audit, reading an appliance rating plate, reacting to an incident sheet that points to carbon monoxide, planning one deck for three products, and correcting a dough temperature from the maintenance log. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
