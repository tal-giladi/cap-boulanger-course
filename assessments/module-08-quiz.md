# Module 8 Quiz: Pain Courant

This quiz checks whether you can run a pain courant batch from the documents a bakery actually uses: scaling a PC-02 order that must also provide tomorrow's pâte fermentée, calculating the water with the pâte fermentée as fourth factor, adjusting pointage to a dough that left the mixer off target, deciding what to do with divided pieces outside tolerance, choosing the home baguette length for an oven, reading a bake log to find why the grignes did not open, judging the end of apprêt, checking salt per 100 g of bread with measured losses, tracing a defect to its stage and writing a useful non-conformity note. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
