# Module 17 Quiz: Hygiene and Food Safety

This quiz checks whether you can use a bakery's food-safety and safety documents to make the right decision: a dairy delivery note with probe readings, a freezer log after a power cut, a crème pâtissière cooling log, a prepacked label, an allergen table against a supplier's technical sheet, a cleaning-plan extract, a manager's note about sick customers, a worker's bench plan, an official recall notice and an extract of the document unique. Each question gives you the document; you choose what the rules and good practice require. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%.
