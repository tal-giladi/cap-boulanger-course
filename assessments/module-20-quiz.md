# Module 20 Quiz: The Bakery as a Workplace and a Business

This quiz checks whether you can use the documents of a bakery's management and of your own working life: a company identity card, a job advert, an employment contract extract, a supply agreement, a timesheet, a payslip, the convention collective's wage grid, an end-of-contract file, a supplier invoice, a price list and a month's simplified results. Each question gives you the document; you choose the reading or the decision that the rules and the figures support. Legal figures are as checked on 2026-10-09. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%.
