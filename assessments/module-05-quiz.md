# Module 5 Quiz: Temperature Management

This quiz checks whether you can manage temperature in realistic bakery situations: calculating the water from a TB printed on a technical sheet, making up the water with ice for a production order, measuring a friction factor with a pre-ferment, reading a temperature log or a cold-room record and deciding what to report, lowering the target for a retarded dough, checking an oven that runs below its dial, keeping a croissant détrempe and its butter at the right temperature, and adapting an apprêt when the cabinet fails. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
