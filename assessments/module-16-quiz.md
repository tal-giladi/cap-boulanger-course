# Module 16 Quiz: Troubleshooting

This quiz checks whether you can go from a symptom to a diagnosis using the documents a bakery keeps: a temperature log on a hot day, production notes after a flour change, an oven log with two decks, a proofing-cabinet record, a croissant non-conformity report, a weighing sheet with ice, a tradition mixing record, a quality check with a core temperature, a bake log of retarded bread and a sheeter note. Each question gives you the symptom and the records; you choose the diagnosis the evidence supports. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%.
