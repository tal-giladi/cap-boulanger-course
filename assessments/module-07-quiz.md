# Module 7 Quiz: The Production Process

This quiz checks whether you can run the production process from realistic documents: placing the oven's preheat in an organigramme, calculating the water for a dough with a pre-ferment, catching a problem on the requisition, diagnosing a dough at shaping from its temperature log, reading scoring and steam results from a bake log, organising the oven loads by deadline, judging a défournement record, conditioning bread for the next day and naming a bread defect from a quality check. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
