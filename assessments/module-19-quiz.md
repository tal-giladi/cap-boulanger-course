# Module 19 Quiz: Communication and Sales

This quiz checks whether you can communicate inside and outside the fournil from the documents a bakery keeps: a delivery note to annotate, a machine fault, draft non-conformity reports, a mill's flour sheet behind a product name, a nutrition analysis, an allergen table at the till, a customer's storage question, a message to a supplier, a technical sheet with blank stage names and a hand-over sheet. Each question gives you the document or the situation; you choose what to say or write. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%.
