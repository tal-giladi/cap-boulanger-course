# Module 3 Quiz: Dough Science

This quiz checks whether you can use module 3 in realistic bakery situations: reading a mixing line on a technical sheet, judging a dough's development and consistency, choosing between slow, improved and intensive mixing and an autolyse, reading a temperature log and deciding what to do with a dough that is too warm, diagnosing over-mixing and over-oxidation from a complaint, and correcting a dough by bassinage or contre-frasage with the right numbers. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
