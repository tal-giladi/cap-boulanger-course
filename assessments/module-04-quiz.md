# Module 4 Quiz: Fermentation

This quiz checks whether you can manage fermentation in realistic bakery situations: adapting pointage and apprêt to the dough temperature in the log, keeping the right quantity of pâte fermentée, judging a poolish plan, respecting the yeast limit for levain bread, organising dividing and shaping, switching to retarded fermentation, reacting to a cold-room or cabinet fault, choosing which load to bake first and diagnosing bread from a bake-off note. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
