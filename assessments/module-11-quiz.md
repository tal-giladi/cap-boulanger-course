# Module 11 Quiz: Viennoiserie: The Croissant

This quiz checks whether you can make croissants from the documents a bakery actually uses: calculating a croissant batch with trims and losses from a production order, setting the water and ice for a cold détrempe with milk in the liquid, refusing an unsuitable fat on a delivery, reading a temperature log during the turns, comparing layer counts on two technical sheets, correcting the sheet thickness from a weighed triangle, choosing a proofer-retarder programme, tracing butter leakage to its cause, writing the allergens on a label and diagnosing a cut section. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
