# Module 18 Quiz: Environmental Responsibility

This quiz checks whether you can apply a bakery's environmental directives from the documents it keeps: an oven log and a draft oven plan, a temperature log of the cold room and freezer, water meter readings, a sales sheet of unsold bread, two businesses with different legal duties, a draft technical sheet that recycles bread, a packaging delivery note, a cleaning-product label and a donation register. Each question gives you the document; you choose the decision the rules and the figures support. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%.
