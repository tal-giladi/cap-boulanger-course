# Module 6 Quiz: Baker's Mathematics

This quiz checks whether you can do the calculations production needs from realistic documents: turning a production order into the flour for a technical sheet, scaling a sheet's base column, finding the largest batch a stock sheet allows, splitting a poolish formula, finding the true hydration of a dough on levain, respecting the yeast limit for levain bread, reading baking losses from a bake log, working back from a baked weight to the pâton, finding the margin from a costing sheet and checking salt per 100 g of bread. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
