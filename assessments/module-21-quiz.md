# Module 21 Quiz: EP1 Practice

This quiz is a short mock of the EP1 written test in multiple-choice form: each question gives you a bakery document (a mill's product sheet, a flour analysis, a technical sheet with temperature readings, a production order, a cooling record, a rating plate, a timesheet, a delivery note) and asks for the decision or the result the rules and the figures support, across professional technology, applied sciences, applied management and the supplies-hygiene-communication competencies. Suggested time limit: 20 minutes, with a calculator and no notes. Legal and regulatory figures are as checked on 2026-10-09. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%.
