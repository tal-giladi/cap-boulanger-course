# Module 10 Quiz: Other Breads

This quiz checks whether you can make the breads beyond the baguette from the documents a bakery actually uses: working back from an ordered baked weight to the pâton for a campagne, calculating the water with a levain as fourth factor, weighing a pain de mie for its tin and putting the lid on at the right moment, diagnosing a campagne made with an over-ripe levain, checking wholemeal rolls against the 1.3 g salt limit, planning rolls and an épi in a one-tray oven, applying the yeast limit of a levain bread, declaring the allergens of a viennois, tracing caved-in pain de mie sides to their cause and correcting the schedule of a seeded dough chilled by its soaker. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
