# Module 14 Quiz: Production Planning

This quiz checks whether you can plan a production from the documents a bakery actually uses: working an order backwards to a start time with two oven loads, correcting a plan from a temperature log with the 7 % rule, spotting a hands conflict in a candidate's organigramme, counting oven loads, choosing one cabinet setting for two products, moving a tradition into a retarded pointage, fixing an oven temperature conflict, placing a crème pâtissière and its cooling on the plan, reporting an equipment failure and adapting a home plan to a hot Israeli kitchen. It has 10 multiple-choice scenario questions; the pass mark is 70%. Keep a calculator at hand.
