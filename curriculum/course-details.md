## One-line summary

From complete beginner to exam-style practice for the French CAP Boulanger: the science, the production and the paperwork, with something to bake or plan at home in every lesson.

## Who it is for

Home cooks with no bakery experience who want to work as a boulanger or sit the CAP Boulanger as an individual candidate.

## What you will learn

- How flour, water, salt, yeast and levain behave, and why.
- How to mix, ferment, shape, score and bake pain courant, pain de tradition française and the other EP2 breads.
- How to laminate croissants, pains au chocolat and pains aux raisins, and make pain au lait and brioche doughs.
- How to calculate formulas, water temperature, yields and costs.
- How to plan several products at once with an organigramme.
- How to judge quality and diagnose faults.
- Hygiene, HACCP, allergens, receiving deliveries, environment and communication with sales staff.
- The workplace and business basics examined in EP1.

## Risk notice (shown to learners before they start)

This course involves hot ovens and steam, hot surfaces, sharp blades, electric mixers and common food allergens; follow each lesson's safety notes and check ingredients for allergies before you bake or share food.
