3. **`references/troubleshooting.md` (optional additions):** Module 4 teaches three fermentation faults that are not rows in that page yet: "Dough rushes, slack, sticky, bland bread → salt forgotten"; "Weak, sticky dough, flat sour bread → over-ripe poolish or pâte fermentée"; "Retarded or blocked dough over-proofed at the set time → cold room/cabinet too warm, dough mixed too warm, too much yeast". Lesson 04.7 covers them if you want to link there.
4. **Rule of thumb used across Module 4:** "about 7 % faster or slower per °C of dough temperature" (yeast activity roughly doubling per 10 °C in the 18-30 °C range), always presented as a planning estimate confirmed by the dough's signs. Module 5 may want to use the same figure for consistency.
