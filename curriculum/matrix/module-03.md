The Module 4 writer (in parallel) may want to check that its pointage numbers agree. No links to Module 4 were made.
4. **`references/troubleshooting.md` (optional additions).** Mixing faults now taught in 03.4/03.5 that could become rows: "very white, bland crumb → over-oxidation (long second speed, delayed salt, bean/soy flour)"; "dough sticky and slack at the divider, warm → over-mixed / dough too warm"; "dry flour specks in crumb → late contre-frasage".
