## Notes for later modules

- The bakery profession's old GBPH (validated 1997) was de-listed in 2025, according to the CNBPF's
  2026 "L'essentiel des bonnes pratiques d'hygiène en boulangerie-pâtisserie"; a new guide had not yet been
  validated by the authorities when checked on 2026-10-08. Module 17 should check the status before
  citing a GBPH.
- Module 1 uses two worked technical sheets: PC-02 (pain courant sur pâte fermentée, T55 100 / water 64 /
  salt 1.8 / fresh yeast 1.5 / pâte fermentée 15) and PD-01 (pain courant direct by hand, T55 100 / water 65 /
  salt 1.8 / fresh yeast 1.5). Later modules may refer to them.
- Lesson 01.7 gives a simplified water-temperature estimate for hand mixing (3 × target − flour − room − 2 °C).
  Module 5 should present the full base-temperature method and can refer back to it.
