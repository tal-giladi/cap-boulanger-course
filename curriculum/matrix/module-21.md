   - Service-Public F10705 CPF (verified 27 June 2026: €500/year, ceiling €5,000; €800/€8,000 without a level-3 qualification; €150 participation; 60/120 days, 30-day reply, silence = acceptance); F14018 projet de transition professionnelle (verified 1 June 2026: 2 years incl. 1 with the employer for a CDI; Transitions Pro; €3,734.04 threshold).
