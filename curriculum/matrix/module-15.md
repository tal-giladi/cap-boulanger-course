5. **Decisions for BUILD_PROGRESS.md.**
   - Crust colour scale 1-5 (pale, blond, golden, golden-brown, dark brown) is a course convention with product targets as in item 1(a); Maillard above about 105 °C, caramelisation 160 °C (ASBE).
   - Crumb recording: holes ≥ 5 mm counted in a 4 × 4 cm card window at the centre of the cross-section; press test (back within about 2 s = elastic); tracing on baking paper. Comparator only, no target numbers.
   - Weight control: sample at least 5 (10 for large batches) from different oven positions, weighed after cooling (home: 1 h); house tolerance ± 3 % of target (matches the rubric); salt checked on the lightest piece. Salt % of dough per sheet: PC-02 1.076, TR-01 1.044, CA-01 1.048, CO-01 0.958, PM-01 0.879, VI-01 1.004 (from modules 8-10).
   - Announced weights: no legal weight per bread type; average kept a few grams above an announced weight (scenario: PC-02 placard 280 g, pâton raised to 355 g; tradition TR-01 300 g pâton, placard 250 g, loss about 16 %).
   - Prepackage tolerances taught from décret 78-166 art. 4 (50-100 g 4.5 g; 100-200 g 4.5 %; 200-300 g 9 g; 300-500 g 3 %; 500-1,000 g 15 g; 1-10 kg 1.5 %). Trade scales: certified, carnet within 1 month, verification every 2 years (direct sale ≤ 30 kg) or yearly (prepackages), green/red sticker (DREETS).
   - Display cooling allowances reused from Module 14: bread about 30 min, viennoiserie about 20 min.
