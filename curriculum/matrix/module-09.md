2. **Numbers kept identical to earlier modules:** tradition extras 2 % bean, 0.5 % soy, 0.3 % wheat malt flour of the total flour; levain: yeast ≤ 0.2 % of the flour at the final mixing; au levain: pH ≤ 4.3, ≥ 900 ppm acetic acid (02.2, 02.7, 02.9, 04.5). Re-verified on Légifrance 2026-10-08: art. 3 amended by décret 97-917 (1997), others unchanged since 1993; the decree does not mention campagne or complet.
3. **Freezing rules (promised by Module 7):** covered in 09.1 (table) and 09.4. Sources: décret 93-1074 art. 2 ("aucun traitement de surgélation au cours de leur élaboration") and Code de la consommation **L122-17** ("La pâte et les pains ne peuvent à aucun stade de la production ou de la vente être surgelés ou congelés", for any business using boulanger/boulangerie; in force since 1 July 2016, https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000032227163). Cold retarding at positive temperature is taught as allowed (référentiel lists pointage retardé au froid for tradition). Module 7 (07.5) can now link to 09.1 where it leaves the tradition freezing rules out.

5. **Consistency decisions to note in BUILD_PROGRESS.md:** (a) Tradition sheet **TR-01**: T65 tradition flour 100, water 70 (68 in the autolyse + 2 eau de réserve by bassinage; Israeli white flour up to 72-74), salt 1.8, fresh yeast 0.6, tradition pâte fermentée 15; total 187.4 %; TPV 23 °C; autolyse 30 min; slow mixing; pointage about 2 h with 2 folds (bakery) or 3 folds at 30/60/90 min (by hand); détente about 30 min; apprêt about 45 min-1 h; bakery pâton 300 g / about 55 cm; home 270 g / about 38 cm, 3 cuts. Salt = 1.044 % of the dough; 1.4 g/100 g reached at about 25 % baking loss. Retarded option: TPV 21-22 °C, yeast about 0.4 %, 45-60 min with two folds, then 12-16 h at 2-6 °C. (b) Home tradition pâte fermentée: mini dough with the same additive-free flour (50 g flour, 35 g water, 0.9 g salt, 0.8 g fresh yeast), evening before, then 75 g (500 g batch) / 150 g (1 kg) kept back; never PC-02 dough in tradition. (c) Four-factor friction factor for TR-01 autolyse + slow mixing in the bakery examples: 14; by hand 8. (d) Home steam exactly as 07.4/08.4. Module 10 and the EP2 practice module can reuse TR-01.
