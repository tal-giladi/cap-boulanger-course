## Requests for main session

1. **Course sheet CR-01 (add to BUILD_PROGRESS decisions; Module 12 reuses it).** Croissant, pâte levée feuilletée. Détrempe: T45 (gruau, or T45 with part gruau) 100, water 26 (cold), whole milk 26 (cold), caster sugar 11, salt 2.0, fresh yeast 4.0 (instant dry: one third), butter in the dough 8 → **détrempe 177 %**; beurre de tourage (butter ≥ 82 %, ideally 84 % beurre sec) 50 → **laminated dough 227 %**. TPV détrempe 20 °C (18-22 °C); short mix; slab 2-3 cm, 2-4 °C at least 2 h, better overnight; lock-in en enveloppe with butter about 13 °C (12-15 °C); **3 tours simples** (27 butter layers; 1 double + 1 simple = 12 as the warm-kitchen alternative), 20-45 min in the fridge between turns; final rest ≥ 1 h or overnight; final sheet **about 3.5 mm**; triangles **11 × 30 cm, about 60 g ± 3 g** (weight ∝ thickness; weigh the first triangle); notch, stretch to about 34 cm, roll loosely, tip under; proof **24-26 °C, 75-80 % HR, never above about 27 °C, about 1 h 30-2 h 30** (2-3 h from the fridge) until almost doubled; two thin coats of egg wash; bake **190-200 °C conventional/deck, 175-180 °C fan, 15-20 min, no steam**, until the creases are gold; planning baking loss about 12-15 % (60 g → 51-53 g). Home batch on 500 g flour: water 130, milk 130, sugar 55, salt 10, fresh yeast 20 (instant 7), butter 40 → détrempe 885 g; beurre de tourage 250 g; **1,135 g**, 16 croissants. Bakery batch 1 kg: 260/260/110/20/40/80 → 1,770 g; butter 500 g; 2,270 g. Batch rule: pieces ÷ 0.90 (trims 10 % of the sheet) × 1.02 (losses) ÷ 2.27. Water: liquid = 3 × TPV − flour − room − friction; water = 2 × liquid − milk temperature (equal weights of water and milk); ice per lesson 05.3.
