## Requests for main session

1. **`curriculum/research/sources.md`: new verified source** (opened with WebFetch on 2026-10-08): BOFiP-Impôts BOI-TVA-LIQ-30-10-10, "TVA – Liquidation – Taux réduits – Produits destinés à l'alimentation humaine", current version in force from 19/11/2025 (https://bofip.impots.gouv.fr/bofip/2033-PGP.html): food for human consumption at 5.5 % (art. 278-0 bis A CGI) with exceptions (confectionery, chocolate products, margarine, caviar, alcohol); food prepared for immediate consumption sold to take away or delivered at 10 % (art. 279 n CGI); bread listed among items at 5.5 %. Used in 06.5; suggest adding it under "Official" for the VAT facts. The service-public "taux de TVA" pages I tried did not cover food rates; impots.gouv's FAQ URL returned 404 and economie.gouv.fr returned 403. Also re-verified on 2026-10-08: King Arthur "Baker's percentage" (overall baker's percent with preferments), King Arthur "French bread with poolish", Ministry of Agriculture salt page, Légifrance décret 93-1074 (art. 4 wording "0,2 p. 100 par rapport au poids de farine mise en oeuvre à ce stade"), référentiel PDF (loads; binary).
2. **Baking-loss figure.** No reputable web source with a baguette baking-loss figure could be verified. The module keeps the course's existing planning figure of about 20 % for baguettes (lesson 02.5) and presents it as a planning estimate, with the learner measuring their own loss in 06.3. If you find an INBP/ANMF or Calvel source, it could be added to 06.3's sources.
3. **New sheet code.** The project introduces two order-specific sheets, PO-01 (baguette sur poolish, overall formula T65 100 / water 68 / salt 1.8 / yeast 0.8, 30 % poolish at 0.2 %) and CA-01 (campagne, T65 85 + rye 15 / water 70 / salt 1.8, 20 % liquid levain), both within `references/formulas.md` ranges. If later modules (8, 9, 10) want running sheets for poolish baguettes or campagne, these could be reused or registered somewhere central.
4. **Forward references to turn into links later** (plain text now because the files do not exist): Module 5 / base-temperature method (06.1, 06.3, 06.4 In Israel notes); lesson 08.3 (06.5 practice, home baguettes).
5. **Back-links for the main session's consideration** (not edited, other modules are off-limits): lesson 01.5 says "module 6 shows how to count the flour and water inside a pre-ferment" and its recipe C answer says "(the levain adds a little more water; module 6)"; lesson 04.5 says "The calculations of pre-ferment formulas come in lesson 06.4". These could become links to `../module-06/lesson-04.md` (06.4 exercise 2 completes recipe C: 73.3 % with a liquid levain, 68.6 % with a firm one).
6. **`references/troubleshooting.md` (optional additions):** "Scaled dough too wet → water taken as a % of dough, or final-dough water not reduced for the poolish" and "Loaves under the ordered baked weight → pâton calculated by adding the loss % instead of dividing by (1 − loss)"; lessons 06.1, 06.3 and 06.4 cover them.
7. **Glossary:** new terms are in `curriculum/glossary-inbox/module-06.md`. Not repeated because already in `glossary.md`: baking loss, bon d'économat, hydratation / taux d'hydratation, levain chef / de tout point, pertes, pourcentage du boulanger, pâte fermentée, poolish, tolérance de poids.
