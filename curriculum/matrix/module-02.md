When Modules 3, 7, 9, 10 and 17 exist, consider turning these into links (`../module-09/lesson-01.md`, etc.).
2. **`references/formulas.md`, "Where the numbers come from":** the salt line says "around 1.8 % of flour weight is the current reference for baguettes". Lesson 02.5 now gives the official source: agreement of 3 March 2022 (DGAL + DGS with the bakery sector), thresholds per 100 g of bread: pains courants 1.5 g (July 2022) then 1.4 g (October 2023); wholemeal/cereal breads 1.3 g (October 2023); pain de mie 1.2 g (October 2023) then 1.1 g (October 2025). Source: https://agriculture.gouv.fr/filiere-boulangerie-vers-une-diminution-du-sel-dans-le-pain-0. You may want to add "1.4 g of salt per 100 g of bread since October 2023" to that line.
3. **`references/formulas.md`, yeast line:** says active dry is "about 40-50%" of fresh. Lesson 02.6 uses instant ≈ 0.33 (Lesaffre: dose 3–4 times lower than pressed yeast) and active dry ≈ 0.4–0.5, consistent with it. No change needed; noted for consistency.
4. **`curriculum/research/sources.md`:** new verified sources used in Module 2 (all opened on 2026-10-08): agriculture.gouv.fr salt agreement page; agriculture.gouv.fr SNANC 2025–2030 page; Lesaffre "different forms of yeast"; King Arthur fresh yeast article; Bake Info pages (describing bread ingredients, yeast, enzymes, flour specifications); INAO Label Rouge specifications LA0514 (farine de meule: flour type/ash table), LR0521 (farine de gruau), LA2006 (farine pour pain courant: additives, ascorbic acid 300 ppm); ANR BAKERY project page; Légifrance arrêté du 21 décembre 2009 (JORFTEXT000021573483) and décret 2015-447 (JORFTEXT000030491684); sante.fr Nutri-Score (20/03/2025) and féculents complets pages; legislation.gov.uk copy of Regulation (EU) 1169/2011 Annex V (EUR-Lex and economie.gouv.fr/DGCCRF returned bot-protection errors from this machine, so they are not cited); École des métiers Dijon butter sheet; Grands Moulins de Paris rye T170 technical sheet; Nomad Education CAP revision page (grain composition).
