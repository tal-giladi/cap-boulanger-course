# Competency matrix — Module 19: Communication and Sales

Codes from `curriculum/competency-map.md`. "Requires physical practice" marks rows whose skill can only be built by doing it: here, speaking French aloud with a partner and recording it, reading a real probe and labels, tasting a real loaf and narrating a real bake. Choosing the right report, name, claim or allergen answer can be practised on paper; saying it fluently to a chef, a seller or a customer is learned by role play at home and on placement.
