3. **`references/formulas.md` (suggested edits):**
   - Pain de mie salt "1.6-1.8": at 1.8 % a lidded loaf with 10 % loss lands at 1.10 g per 100 g, exactly the 1.1 g limit, and over it above 10 % loss. Module 10 uses **1.6 %** (0.98 g). Suggest "1.5-1.6 (limit 1.1 g per 100 g since October 2025; 1.8 is already at the limit)".
   - Pain complet salt "1.8-2.0": at 1.8 % a 60 g wholemeal roll with 23 % loss reaches 1.32 g per 100 g, over the 1.3 g limit for wholemeal and cereal breads. Module 10 uses **1.7 %**. Suggest "1.6-1.8 (limit 1.3 g per 100 g; check small pieces)" and adding the 1.3 g limit to the "Where the numbers come from" section.
   - Pain viennois salt "2.0": module 10 uses **1.8 %** (1.17 g per 100 g for a 280 g baguette viennoise at 14 % loss; 2.0 % gives 1.30 g). Viennois is not named in the salt agreement's categories; suggest "1.8-2.0".
   - New course sheets defined in module 10, which later modules can reuse (add to BUILD_PROGRESS decisions): **CO-01** pain complet (T150 100, water 74, salt 1.7, fresh yeast 1.8; TPV 25 °C); **PM-01** pain de mie (T55 100, water 60, milk powder 4, salt 1.6, fresh yeast 3.5, sugar 5, butter 8; total 182.1; TPV 25 °C; pâton = tin volume × 0.35 g/cm³; baking loss about 10 %); **VI-01** pain viennois (T45/T55 100, whole milk 58, salt 1.8, fresh yeast 3.5, sugar 6, butter 10; total 179.3; TPV 25 °C; 180-200 °C, no steam when egg-washed); **SE-01** seeded loaf (T65 100, water 66, salt 1.8, fresh yeast 1.5, soaker seeds 20 + water 25; total 214.3); CA-01 reused from the Module 6 workbook (home levain build: 100 g T65, 100 g water, 20 g seed).
4. **`references/troubleshooting.md` (optional additions):** "Fendu closed, no split → groove too shallow / not floured / proofed groove up (10.1)"; "Tabatière lid stuck → flap too thick, edge not oiled (10.1)"; "Épi leaves fell off / closed → cut right through / only half through (10.2)"; "Pain de mie sides caved in → unmoulded late or under-baked (10.5)"; "Pain de mie rounded corners or lid forced → dough weight not matched to the tin (10.5)"; "Viennois streaky dull crust → steam on egg wash (10.6)"; "Seeded bread dry, stales fast → seeds added dry (10.7)"; "Campagne slack, very sour → over-ripe levain (10.3)"; "Rolls or small wholemeal pieces over the salt limit → high baking loss (10.2, 10.4)".
