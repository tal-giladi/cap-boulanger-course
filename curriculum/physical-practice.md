# Competencies that require physical practice

Everything below can be started at home with this course, but reaching exam standard needs hands-on
hours, ideally in a professional fournil (PFMP, job, or a training centre). Marked "requires physical
practice" in the matrix.

| Competency | Why online is not enough | Home pathway in this course | What still needs a real bakery |
|---|---|---|---|
| C2.2 Peser, mesurer | Speed and accuracy under time pressure | Modules 1, 6, 8 | Weighing 4–6 kg batches quickly |
| C2.3 Préparer, fabriquer (bread) | Feel of dough, shaping speed, scoring | Modules 3, 4, 7–10 | Spiral mixer, deck oven, 60+ baguettes per batch |
| C2.3 Préparer, fabriquer (viennoiserie) | Lamination by hand, sheeter use | Modules 11–12 | Laminoir (sheeter) — required for the final sheet in EP2 |
| C2.4 Conditionner | Real packaging, labelling | Modules 12, 18 | Shop packaging lines |
| C2.5 Employee health and safety | Real machines | Modules 1, 13, 17 | Spiral mixer, divider, sheeter, oven loader safety |
| C2.8 Disposer les produits au magasin | Real shop | Module 15 | Working with a sales team |
| C3.2 Contrôler poids, quantités, aspect | Volume of products | Modules 6, 15 | Batch weight control |
| C1.2 Organiser son travail | Real time pressure, shared equipment | Modules 14, 22 | Full 6 h 30 practical with professional equipment |
| C2.1 / C3.1 Receiving deliveries | Real deliveries | Module 17 | PFMP or job |
| C4.4 Reporting malfunctions | Real team | Module 19 | PFMP or job |

PFMP: 14 weeks in the arrêté (school pathway). Individual candidates: check the académie's rules
(see `references/cap-exam.md`).
