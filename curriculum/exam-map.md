EP2 marking grid (REF): calculation and organisation 20; work during the test / production 90; hygiene
and safety 20; appearance of finished products 90; tasting 20; total 240.

EP2 products (REF), the candidate makes according to the subject:

1. Pain de tradition française or pain courant (mixing method of the candidate's choice), from 4–6 kg
   flour with pâte fermentée supplied: baguettes including épi; long loaves; shaped pieces in 2 forms;
   petits pains in 3 forms of the candidate's choice.
2. Other bread from 1 kg flour (one of): pain complet, pain viennois, pain de mie, pain de campagne.
3. Pâte levée feuilletée from 1 kg flour: 12 croissants, 12 pains au chocolat, 12 pains aux raisins
   (crème pâtissière supplied).
4. Pâte levée from 1 kg flour (one of): pain au lait or pain brioché, as large pieces (one kind of
   braid, 1–3 strands) and small pieces (navettes and braids, or animals).

Rules: mixing by machine; shaping by hand; laminated dough: beurrage and tourage with the rolling pin,
final sheet on the sheeter.

Admission (RNCP, SIEC): diploma awarded with an average ≥ 10/20 over all tests; SIEC (Île-de-France)
states candidates need the general average AND the professional-domain average. Blocks scored ≥ 10/20
are kept as block attestations.
