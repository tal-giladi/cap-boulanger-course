# Official competency map — CAP Boulanger (RNCP42115)

Source: France Compétences RNCP42115 (checked 2026-10-08) and the référentiel annexed to the arrêté
of 21 February 2014 (amended 22 July 2019). See `curriculum/research/sources.md`.

## Blocks (RNCP42115)

| Block | Title | In this course? |
|---|---|---|
| BC01 | Approvisionnement, communication, sécurité alimentaire et hygiène en boulangerie | Yes (assessed by EP1) |
| BC02 | Production et présentation de produits de panification et de boulangerie | Yes (assessed by EP2) |
| BC03 | Français et histoire-géographie-enseignement moral et civique | No — additional preparation required |
| BC04 | Mathématiques et physique-chimie | No (bakery maths only) — additional preparation required |
| BC05 | Langue vivante étrangère (A2) | No — additional preparation required |
| BC06 | Éducation physique et sportive | No — additional preparation required |
| BC07 | Prévention-santé-environnement | Partly (occupational risk, hygiene, first aid pointers) — additional preparation required |

| S2.3 | Products for savoury fillings: fresh, frozen, vacuum-packed, canned, sauces | 2, 17 |
