## Notes on conflicts (decided)

- PFMP: original 2014 referentiel says 16 weeks; Légifrance consolidated art. 3 (amended 22/07/2019) says
  **14 weeks**. Use 14 weeks, cite ARRETE.
- EP2 coefficient: referentiel says 13 "including 1 for PSE" and 8 h "including 1 h PSE". Since the
  2019 general-units arrêté (UG-2019), PSE is a separate general unit (coef 1). Official exam subjects
  after 2019 show EP2 = coef 12, 7 h (30 min written + 6 h 30 practical). Teach: EP1 coef 4, 2 h
  written; EP2 coef 12, 7 h; PSE separate coef 1. Always add: "confirm on your convocation".
- Chef-d'œuvre: applies to school pupils and apprentices only (SIEC); not to individual candidates.
- Registration and PFMP evidence rules vary by académie: always tell learners to check their
  académie's exam division (DEC/SIEC) page.
