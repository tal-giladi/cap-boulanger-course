# Troubleshooting

One page for diagnosing bread and viennoiserie faults: problem → probable causes → evidence to look for → correction now → prevention next time. Change one thing at a time, and write the change in your [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md). Module 16 teaches the method in detail.

## Method

1. Describe the fault precisely (where, how much, every piece or only some).
2. Check the records: dough temperature, times, weights, oven temperature.
3. List the probable causes from the tables below and look for the evidence that separates them.
4. Correct what you can now; write down the prevention for next time.

## Bread

| Problem | Probable causes | Evidence | Correction now | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dense crumb, low volume | Under-fermented; dough too cold; too little yeast; under-mixed weak dough | Dough temperature below target; tight, small holes; heavy loaf | Extend apprêt if not yet baked | Hit target dough temperature; judge fermentation by the dough, not the clock |
| Loaf spreads flat | Over-fermented; dough too soft (too much water); weak flour; slack shaping | Sour smell, collapsing when scored, large irregular holes at top | Bake immediately, hotter, no long wait | Shorter pointage or cooler dough; less water; more tension in shaping |
| Tears on the side (pain cintré, bursting) | Under-proofed; scoring too shallow; too little steam | Wild tear along the bottom edge; scores closed | — | Longer apprêt; deeper, more horizontal blade; steam at loading |
| Scores do not open | Over-proofed; blade held vertical; skin dried (pâte croûtée); no steam | Flat cuts, no ear; dry dull skin | — | Cover dough while proofing; 30-45° blade angle; steam |
| Pale crust | Over-fermented (sugars used up); oven too cool; too short a bake | Pale even with long bake; dull colour | Bake longer | Shorter fermentation; check oven with a thermometer |
| Crust too dark or burnt | Oven too hot; too much sugar or malt; bake too long | Dark bottom (pain ferré) when sole too hot | — | Lower sole heat; check timer; use a second tray under |
| Dull crust (croûte terne) | No or too little steam; over-proofed; dough skinned | Matte grey crust | — | Steam at loading; cover dough while proofing |
| Blisters on crust (pain cloqué) | Too much steam; very long cold fermentation; dough too wet on surface | Small bubbles on the crust | — | Less steam; vent the oven at the end of the bake |
| Gummy crumb | Under-baked; cut while hot; too much water | Damp sticky crumb near the base | Return to oven | Bake to core 96-98 °C; cool fully before cutting |
| Dry, crumbly crumb | Too little water; over-baked; old flour; staled | Crumbles when sliced | — | Correct hydration; shorter bake; store correctly |
| Large irregular holes or tunnels | Poor degassing at shaping; flour trapped in folds; over-proofed | Tunnel under the crust | — | Even shaping; brush off excess flour |
| Weak oven spring | Over-proofed; oven not hot enough; no steam; weak flour | Low volume, flat top | — | Shorter apprêt; preheat 45-60 min; steam |

## Viennoiserie

| Problem | Probable causes | Evidence | Correction now | Prevention |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Butter leaks during baking | Proofed too warm (above about 27 °C); under-proofed; layers torn | Pool of butter on the tray; fried bottoms | — | Proof at 24-26 °C; give full proof; keep dough and butter cold during turns |
| Butter breaks into pieces | Butter too cold and hard compared with the dough | Visible butter flakes and patches in the sheet | Rest dough a few minutes at room temperature before the next turn | Butter and dough at similar firmness (butter about 12-15 °C) |
| Butter melts into the dough | Butter or room too warm; too long between turns | Greasy dough, no distinct layers | Chill 20-30 min before continuing | Work cold; chill between turns |
| Uneven layers | Uneven rolling; butter not square; corners not aligned | Thick and thin areas when cut | — | Roll evenly in both directions; square the butter plaque |
| Poor lift, dense interior | Too many turns (layers merge); under-proofed; dough too strong | Bready crumb, small volume | — | Correct number of turns; full proof |
| Distorted shapes | Dough not rested before cutting; uneven cutting | Croissants shrink or bend | — | Rest the sheet before cutting; use a template |
| Pale or patchy colour | Egg wash missing or uneven; oven too cool | Pale patches | — | Two thin coats of egg wash; check temperature |
