# Troubleshooting

One page for diagnosing bread and viennoiserie faults: problem → probable causes → evidence to look for → correction now → prevention next time → the lesson that teaches the cause. Change one thing at a time, and write the change in your [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md). What a good product looks like, outside and in, is in lessons [15.1](../lessons/module-15/lesson-01.md) and [15.2](../lessons/module-15/lesson-02.md). Module 16 teaches the method in detail: [16.1 · A Method for Diagnosis](../lessons/module-16/lesson-01.md).

## Method

1. Describe the fault precisely (where, how much, every piece or only some).
2. Check the records: dough temperature, times, weights, oven temperature.
3. List the probable causes from the tables below and look for the evidence that separates them.
4. Correct what you can now; write down the prevention for next time.

A fault on **every piece** points to a cause shared by the whole dough (formula, flour, dough temperature, pointage, oven setting). A fault on **only some pieces** points to order, place or waiting (last pieces shaped, one tray or shelf, an oven zone, the second load). If two causes are proved, correct the earlier stage first. Report production faults with the [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md).

The numbers used below are the course's: fermentation about 7 % faster or slower per °C of dough temperature ([04.2](../lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)); bread apprêt about 24-28 °C and 75-85 % RH; croissant proof 24-26 °C, never above about 27 °C; lamination butter about 13 °C (plastic about 12-15 °C); home steam = metal tray preheated on the lowest shelf, 100-150 mL hot water at loading, fan off, steam out at about 10 minutes ([08.4](../lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)); core at least 93 °C, baguettes usually 96-98 °C.

## Dough and mixing

| Problem | Probable causes | Evidence | Correction now | Prevention | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dough springs back, shrinks, tears at shaping (excès de force) | Strong flour or ascorbic acid; firm dough; many folds or long pointage; cold dough; intensive mixing | Flour ingredients list; hydration; shaping notes | Longer détente (10-15 min more); looser pre-shape; shape in two passes | Autolyse; 1-2 points more water; fewer folds | [03.5](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md), [16.2](../lessons/module-16/lesson-02.md) |
| Dough slackens and spreads (manque de force) | Weak flour; too much water; over-mixed (letdown); salt forgotten; over-ripe pre-ferment; warm dough | Hydration; mixing time; salt tick; pre-ferment state | Fold during pointage; tighter shaping; shorter apprêt; support (couche, banneton) | Stronger flour; less water; stop mixing at the optimum | [03.5](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md), [16.2](../lessons/module-16/lesson-02.md) |
| Dough too firm (pâte trop ferme) | Water short for this flour; new, thirstier flour | Mixer labours; dough tears | Bassinage in small additions | Hold back 2-3 % water and finish by feel | [03.5](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md), [07.2](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md) |
| Dough too soft (pâte trop douce) | Too much water; water taken as % of dough; ice weighed on top of the water | Weighing sheet | Contre-frasage before full development; folds | Water from flour %; ice counted inside the water weight | [05.3](../lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md), [06.1](../lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md) |
| Very white, cottony, bland crumb | Over-oxidation: long second speed, delayed salt, bean or soy flour | Mixing times and speeds; salt timing | — | Shorter or slower mixing; salt in during frasage; autolyse | [03.4](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md), [16.4](../lessons/module-16/lesson-04.md) |
| Dough sticky and slack at the divider, warm | Over-mixed and/or dough too warm | Dough temperature; mixing time | Divide quickly; firm shaping; cooler, shorter proof | Stop on the windowpane; correct the water temperature | [03.4](../lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md) |
| Dry flour specks in the crumb | Contre-frasage too late, after development | Specks spread through the crumb (not along folds) | Mix a little longer in first speed | Correct consistency at the end of frasage | [03.5](../lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md) |
| Dough far too warm in a dough with a pre-ferment | 3-factor friction factor used in a 4-factor calculation, or pre-ferment temperature not counted | Calculation on the sheet | Shorter pointage (7 % rule); report | One convention per product, written on the sheet | [05.3](../lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md) |
| Dough far too soft after cooling the water with ice | Ice added on top of the full water weight | Weighing sheet | Contre-frasage; folds | Ice is part of the water: total stays the same | [05.3](../lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md) |
| Dough several degrees warmer than calculated | Friction factor of another product, method or batch size; second speed too long | Mixer, batch size, timings | Shorten pointage (7 % rule) | One friction factor per product and batch size | [13.1](../lessons/module-13/lesson-01.md) |
| Scaled dough too wet | Water taken as a % of the dough, or final-dough water not reduced for the poolish | Calculation on the sheet | Hold back water if not yet added; otherwise extra flour, noted | Water × flour; final water = total water − pre-ferment water | [06.1](../lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md), [06.4](../lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md) |
| Dough rushes, sticky, slack, bland | Salt forgotten | Salt not ticked; taste the dough | If not yet divided, salt can sometimes be worked in; otherwise report | Tick salt at weighing; salt in its own container | [04.7](../lessons/module-04/lesson-07.md), [07.2](../lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md) |

## Fermentation and proofing

| Problem | Probable causes | Evidence | Correction now | Prevention | Lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weak, sticky dough; flat, sour bread | Over-ripe poolish or pâte fermentée | Pre-ferment collapsed, alcohol or vinegar smell; age and temperature | Use less of it, mix cooler, shorten pointage | Respect time, yeast and temperature of the pre-ferment; keep pâte fermentée at about 4 °C, dated | [04.5](../lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md) |
| Retarded or blocked dough over-proofed at the set time | Cold room or cabinet too warm; dough mixed too warm; too much yeast | Cabinet or cold-room record; TPV | Divide or bake at once; short apprêt | Cooler TPV, less yeast; check the cold temperature | [04.6](../lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md) |
| Blocked pieces over-proofed in the morning | Cabinet never reached blocking temperature: dirty condenser, next to the oven, overloaded | Cabinet's own record | Bake early, or reshape if possible | Clean, ventilated condenser away from the oven; read the record each morning | [13.3](../lessons/module-13/lesson-03.md) |
| Second oven load over-proofed | Both groups proofed at the same temperature, load 2 waiting about 30 min more | Apprêt time per load | Bake it at once; note the defect | Proof group 2 about 3-4 °C cooler (7 % rule) or shape it later | [14.1](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md) |
| Dough ready, oven cold | Preheat not on the plan | Organigramme | Hold the pieces cooler while the oven heats | "Oven on" as a line on the organigramme, 45-60 min before the first load | [07.1](../lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md), [14.1](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md) |

| Cuts open as flat grooves, no ear | Blade held upright or cut too deep | Symmetrical grooves | — | Blade flat at about 30°, about 5 mm deep | [08.4](../lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md) |
| Cuts flat and fused | Cuts too short or not overlapping; too much steam | Cut pattern | Open the ouras or door earlier | Cuts overlapping by a third; steam only at loading | [07.4](../lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md) |
| Cuts like a barber's pole | Cuts across the loaf instead of along the midline | Cut direction | — | Cuts nearly parallel to the length, close to the midline | [08.4](../lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md) |
| Seam opens in apprêt or in the oven | Seam not sealed; flour in the seam | Seam position | Pinch closed before proofing | Seal with the heel of the hand along the whole length | [08.3](../lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md) |
| Weak oven spring | Over-proofed; oven not hot enough; no steam; weak flour | Low volume, flat top | — | Shorter apprêt; preheat 45-60 min; steam | [07.5](../lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md), [13.4](../lessons/module-13/lesson-04.md) |
| Baguettes torn by the façonneuse | Excès de force; détente too short; rollers too tight | First piece out of the moulder | Longer détente; open the gap one notch | Settings matched to each dough | [07.3](../lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md), [13.2](../lessons/module-13/lesson-02.md) |
| Tradition spreads flat in the couche | Seam not sealed; over-fermented | Seam; poke test | Bake slightly early; firmer support with the couche folds | Seal firmly; check hydration and pointage | [09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md) |
