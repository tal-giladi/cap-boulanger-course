# The CAP Boulanger Exam

Curriculum version: RNCP42115, checked 2026-10-08. This page is the only place in the course that states exam rules; lessons link here. Rules can change, so confirm every detail with your académie's exam office and your convocation before you sit the exam.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This free course prepares you for the CAP Boulanger. It is not the CAP itself and does not award the French national diploma. The CAP is a French national diploma awarded by the Ministry of Education (Ministère de l'Éducation nationale) after the official examination.

## EP1: written test on technology, applied science and management

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Unit | UP1, coefficient 4 |
| Form for individual candidates and distance learners | Written, one-off (ponctuel écrit), 2 hours, marked out of 80 |
| Form for school pupils, apprentices in approved centres, public adult training | Continuous assessment (CCF), same requirements |
| Structure | One realistic company situation shared by three parts |
| Part 1: professional technology (S1, S2, S3) | about 45 min, /30 |
| Part 2: applied sciences (S4: food, hygiene, work environment) | about 45 min, /30 |
| Part 3: applied management (S5, at least 3 of 5 areas) | about 30 min, /20 |
| Competencies | C1.1, C2.1, C2.3, C2.5, C2.6, C2.7, C3.1, C4.1, C4.2, C4.3 |

Real EP1 papers use company documents (technical sheets, protocols, analysis results, articles) and ask for definitions, explanations and justified decisions. Past papers are published by the Base nationale des sujets d'examens ([example: session 2019](https://bnseep.eduscol.education.fr/ressources/examens/sujets/19/500/2213700/EP1/UP1_SUJET.pdf)). Module 21 of this course practises EP1.

[Module 21](../lessons/module-21/lesson-01.md) of this course practises EP1 round by round and ends with a [full mock paper](../projects/m21-ep1-mock-paper.md).

## EP2: production

| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Unit | UP2, coefficient 12 |
| Form for individual candidates and distance learners | One-off written and practical test, 7 hours |
| Written phase | 30 minutes: from an order, complete the technical sheet (pain courant or tradition) and the work organigramme for the whole order. Your own recipe notebook is allowed. |
| Practical phase | 6 hours 30 minutes: make and present the ordered products following hygiene and safety rules |
| Marking (out of 240) | Calculation and organisation 20; work during the test 90; hygiene and safety 20; appearance of finished products 90; tasting 20 |
| Form for school pupils and apprentices | CCF: one assessment in the company (60 points) and one in the training centre (180 points) |
| Competencies | C1.2, C1.3, C2.2, C2.3, C2.4, C2.5, C2.6, C2.7, C2.8, C3.2, C4.4 |

### Products in EP2 (as defined in the référentiel)

1. **Pain de tradition française or pain courant** (mixing method of your choice), from 4 to 6 kg of flour, with pâte fermentée supplied by the exam centre: baguettes including an épi; long loaves; shaped pieces in 2 different forms; petits pains (rolls) in 3 forms of your choice.
2. **Another bread** from 1 kg of flour, one of: pain complet, pain viennois, pain de mie or pain de campagne (type, weight and shapes given in the subject).
3. **Pâte levée feuilletée** (laminated yeast dough) from 1 kg of flour: 12 croissants, 12 pains au chocolat and 12 pains aux raisins (crème pâtissière supplied).
4. **Pâte levée** (enriched yeast dough) from 1 kg of flour, one of: pain au lait or pain brioché, presented as large pieces (one kind of braid, 1 to 3 strands) and small pieces (navettes and braids, or animals).

Rules during the test: mixing is done by machine; shaping is done by hand; for laminated dough the butter is locked in and the turns are given with the rolling pin, and the final sheet is rolled on the sheeter (laminoir).

> [!WARNING]
> Bring the protective equipment named on your convocation. The Île-de-France exam service ([SIEC](https://siec.education.fr/candidats/examens/CAP/tout-savoir-sur-le-cap/cap-tout-savoir-sur-la-session)) states that a candidate without the required protective equipment gets 0/20 in a practical test.

[Module 22](../lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md) of this course rehearses EP2 at home. A home rehearsal is not the exam: you will still need practice on professional equipment (spiral mixer, deck oven, sheeter).

## Passing

- The diploma is awarded with an average of at least 10/20 over all tests ([RNCP42115](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rncp/42115)). The SIEC states that candidates must have both the general average and the average in the professional domain.
- A block scored at least 10/20 (or validated through VAE) gives a block attestation. A candidate who already holds blocks can be exempted from the matching tests.
- The optional language test only counts points above 10.

## Ways to get the CAP

| Route | Who | Assessment |
|---|---|---|
| School (lycée professionnel) | Pupils | CCF; includes 14 weeks of workplace training (PFMP) |
| Apprenticeship (CFA) | Apprentices with an employer | CCF in approved centres, one-off tests otherwise |
| Adult training (formation continue) | Adults | CCF in public centres, one-off tests in private centres |
| Individual candidate (candidat libre) and distance learning | Anyone who registers alone | One-off tests |
| Candidates with 3 years of professional experience | Experienced workers | One-off tests |
| VAE (validation des acquis de l'expérience) | Experienced workers | Validation of experience instead of tests |

Sources: [RNCP42115](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rncp/42115) and the [arrêté](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000028699556) (PFMP of 14 weeks, article 3 as amended in 2019).

## Administrative requirements for individual candidates

Rules are set by each académie. Example from the Île-de-France exam service (SIEC) for the 2027 session:

- Registration online (Cyclades) from 14 October to 18 November 2026, with the signed file sent by 23 November 2026; no late registration.
- One CAP specialty per year, in one académie.
- You must be an adult (18) by 31 December of the exam year; under-25s add their Journée défense et citoyenneté certificate.
- Some CAP specialties require signed workplace training certificates at registration. Check whether your académie asks this for the CAP Boulanger and which model certificate it uses.

Other académies have similar but not identical dates and rules. Search your académie's site for "candidats individuels CAP" or contact its exam division (DEC).

## Exemptions from the general subjects

The SIEC states: only diplomas from the European Union give exemptions from general tests; only French vocational, agricultural or maritime diplomas also exempt from PSE; only a level 4 diploma or a CAP gives exemptions; a diploma from outside the EU gives no exemption. Ask your académie for the exemption table before you register.
