# Project: Multi-Product Schedule

A Sunday order in a craft bakery mixes four doughs, two ovens, one proofing cabinet and one baker, and only a plan made on Saturday afternoon gets it into the shop by 7:00. In Part A you plan that order completely: the batches, what moves to Saturday with the cold, the oven plan, the two organigrammes, the resource check and two "what if" lines, including a report to the chef. In Part B you plan and run a multi-product home bake from your own organigramme and compare the clock with your plan. The Academy cannot grade your plan, your bread or your photos: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference answers; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Commande de production, dimanche**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Poids pâton | Fiche | Pour |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Baguettes (pain courant) | 30 | 350 g | PC-02 | magasin 7:00 |
> | Petits pains ronds (pain courant) | 20 | 60 g | PC-02 | restaurant Le Zinc 7:30 |
> | Baguettes de tradition | 20 | 300 g | TR-01, pointage retardé | magasin 7:00 |
> | Croissants | 40 | 60 g | CR-01 | magasin 7:00 |
> | Pains au chocolat | 30 | 58 g + 2 bâtons | CR-01 | magasin 7:00 |
> | Tresses à trois branches | 2 | 300 g | PB-01 | magasin 7:00 |
> | Navettes | 20 | 50 g | PB-01 | magasin 7:00 |
>
> Pertes de fabrication 2 %; farine arrondie à la centaine de grammes supérieure. CR-01: pâte à tourer = pièces ÷ 0,90 × 1,02; détrempe 177 %, beurrage 50 % de la farine (227 %). TR-01 en pointage retardé: TPV 21-22 °C, levure 0,4 %, 45-60 min avec deux rabats, puis 12-16 h à 2-6 °C; division à froid, détente 45-60 min. PB-01: TPV 23 °C, 30 min avec un rabat, puis au froid.
> Vous êtes seul. Samedi: 12:30-19:00 disponible. Dimanche: arrivée au plus tôt à 2:00.
> Équipement: un pétrin à spirale; four à soles, 2 soles à thermostats indépendants (12 baguettes, 20 petits pains ou 2 plaques 40 × 60 par sole), 60 min pour monter à 250 °C; four ventilé 5 plaques (20 min pour 175 °C); armoire de pousse programmable 16 niveaux (un seul programme à la fois); chambre froide 3 °C; fournil 22 °C. Pâte fermentée PC-02 du samedi en chambre froide.

In English: 30 pain courant baguettes and 20 rolls, 20 tradition baguettes made with a retarded pointage, 40 croissants and 30 pains au chocolat, and a pain brioché in two braids and 20 navettes. You work alone, with Saturday afternoon to prepare and a start no earlier than 2:00 on Sunday. The equipment, the course sheets and the rules for batches are given.

## You need

- Part A: calculator; four copies of the [production sheet template](../templates/production-sheet.md); two copies of the [organigramme template](../templates/organigramme.md) (Saturday, Sunday); the [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md); lessons [14.1](../lessons/module-14/lesson-01.md)-[14.4](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md) and the sheets in lessons [01.6](../lessons/module-01/lesson-06.md) (PC-02), [09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md) (TR-01), [11.2](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md) (CR-01) and [12.3](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md) (PB-01).
- Part B: the home equipment of the lessons for the doughs you choose, an oven thermometer, a timer, the [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md), the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md) and the [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md).
- Time: Part A about 2 hours; Part B a full day (plus the evening before for a pâte fermentée or a retarded dough).

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses an oven at up to 250 °C with steam, a lame and knives, and (if you make crème pâtissière) boiling milk. Steam only into a preheated metal tray, never a glass dish; dry oven gloves; stand back when you open the door. Cut away from your fingers. A schedule that makes you run is a safety problem: if two hot jobs collide, stop and finish one.

> [!CAUTION]
> Allergens in these products: wheat (gluten), milk, egg (dough and egg wash), soy and milk in most chocolate. Raw egg: wash hands after shells, keep egg wash cold and discard it after use. If you make crème pâtissière, cool it from 63 °C to 10 °C in 2 hours or less and discard it after 24 hours.

## Steps

### Part A — the Sunday order (about 2 hours)

1. **Batches.** For each dough: dough needed, with 2 %; flour rounded up; every ingredient; check the total. For TR-01 use the retarded yeast (0.4 %). Count the chocolate batons.
2. **Rank** the doughs by start-to-oven time. Decide which work moves to Saturday afternoon and with which cold technique (lessons [04.6](../lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md), [14.2](../lessons/module-14/lesson-02.md)).
3. **Oven plan** for the deck oven and the fan oven: every load with product, deck or oven, temperature, time in and out, and the time each product is cooled. Preheat times.
4. **Saturday organigramme** from 12:30: détrempe, lamination, shaping, the TR-01 mix and its time into the cold room, the PB-01 mix, shaping and blocking, the cabinet programme with its ready time, cleaning.
5. **Sunday organigramme** backwards from the oven plan, with the hands column.
6. **Resource check** (lesson [14.3](../lessons/module-14/lesson-03.md)): hands, mixer, ovens (temperature and capacity), cabinet (one setting, levels), cold room. Name the busiest half-hour.
7. **What if (a):** on Sunday the PC-02 dough comes off the mixer at 26.5 °C instead of 24 °C. Write the new pointage and what you change.
8. **What if (b):** at 4:00 the bottom deck will not heat. Work out what can still be ready at 7:00 and write a three-line report to the chef, then fill in a non-conformity report (C4.4).
9. **Fill in** the four production sheets and the two organigrammes.

### Part B — your home multi-product day

10. **Choose** at least two doughs with different ovens: for example TR-01 tradition baguettes and a CR-01 lamination (croissants and pains au chocolat), or the three-dough day of lesson [14.4](../lessons/module-14/lesson-04.md). Use home batches from the lessons.
11. **Measure your oven** first: minutes from cold to 250 °C, from 250 to 200 °C with the door open.
12. **Plan on paper** before the day: production sheets, the oven queue (hottest first), the organigramme with hands, fridge and proofing-place columns, and the time of every check (dough temperatures, poke tests, cream log if any).
13. **Run the day.** At the end of every stage write the real clock time next to the planned time, with the dough temperature after each mixing.
14. **Evaluate** the products after cooling with the quality rubric, and weigh them.
15. **Write three lines:** the stage that drifted most and why (temperature? hands? oven?), what you did about it, and the one change to your next plan.

## Deliverables

- Part A: the batches of step 1 and the baton count; the ranking and the Saturday decision; the oven plan; two organigrammes; the resource check with the busiest half-hour; the two "what if" answers, the report to the chef and the non-conformity report; four production sheets.
- Part B: your oven measurements, the production sheets, the organigramme with planned and real times side by side, the temperature log, the bake log with weights, the rubric scores and your three lines.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Batches | Each dough with 2 % and rounding; CR-01 ÷ 0.90 × 1.02 ÷ 2.27; TR-01 with 0.4 % yeast | A product forgotten (rolls, Sunday navettes); laminated dough divided by 1.77 |
| Use of the cold | Lamination, shaping and PB-01 on Saturday with blocking; TR-01 retarded 12-16 h | Croissants started on Sunday; TR-01 same day from midnight |
| Oven plan | Written before the doughs; one temperature per deck or oven; every load cooled before its deadline | Mixed temperatures on one deck; cooling forgotten |
| Organigrammes | Built backwards from the loads; hands never doubled; preheat, cleaning and egg wash placed | Forward plan; two shaping blocks overlapping |
| Resource check | Cabinet setting shared and below 27 °C for viennoiserie; levels counted; busiest half-hour named | Cabinet set warmer for bread; no capacity check |
| What if and report | Pointage recalculated with the 7 % rule; deck failure translated into what is late, by how much, with options; non-conformity report filled | "Work faster"; the chef told at 6:55 |
| Home plan vs reality | Every stage within about 15 minutes, or the drift explained by a measured cause | No real times recorded |
| Home products | Each product within its weight tolerance and scored with the rubric | No weights; no rubric |
| Safety and hygiene | Steam, blades, eggs and cream handled as in the warnings; allergens listed | Steps skipped |
