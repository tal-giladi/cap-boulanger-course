# Project: Read and Prepare a Production Order

This project brings module 1 together. You take a realistic bakery order, turn it into a full preparation file (ingredient weights, requisition, equipment, sequence, fermentation times, baking plan and start time), then carry out a home-sized order from start to finish and record it as a professional would.

## Brief

You are the ouvrier boulanger on the early shift at Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle. The chef leaves you this order and the technical sheet PC-02 (in [lesson 01.6](../lessons/module-01/lesson-06.md)).

> **Commande de production — mardi 24 novembre 2026**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Poids pâton | Pour |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Baguette (pain courant) | 36 | 350 g | magasin 6:45 |
> | Bâtard (pain courant) | 6 | 400 g | magasin 7:30 |
> | Petit pain rond (pain courant) | 30 | 60 g | Hôtel Le Relais, livraison 7:15 |
>
> Une seule pâte, fiche PC-02 (farine T55 100 %, eau 64 %, sel 1,8 %, levure fraîche 1,5 %, pâte fermentée 15 %). Pertes: 2 %. Arrondir la farine à la centaine de grammes supérieure.
>
> Matériel: pétrin à spirale (capacité 25 kg de pâte), four à sole 3 étages (un étage = 12 baguettes, ou 8 bâtards, ou 30 petits pains), chambre de pousse.
>
> Temps de la fiche: pesée 15 min; pétrissage 10 min; pointage 45 min; division et boulage 25 min; détente 20 min; façonnage 35 min; apprêt 1 h 15 à 25 °C; cuisson baguettes 22 min, bâtards 28 min, petits pains 15 min à 250 °C avec buée; ressuage 30 min minimum.

Part A is paperwork for this order. Part B is a home order you bake yourself:

> **Commande maison:** 12 petits pains ronds, pâton 70 g, fiche PD-01 (lesson [01.6](../lessons/module-01/lesson-06.md): farine T55 100 %, eau 65 %, sel 1,8 %, levure fraîche 1,5 %), prêts à 13:00.

## You need

- Calculator, paper or a file.
- [Production sheet template](../templates/production-sheet.md), [organigramme template](../templates/organigramme.md), [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md), [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md).
- For Part B: the equipment and ingredients of [lesson 01.7](../lessons/module-01/lesson-07.md).

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses a hot oven and steam. Follow the safety steps of lesson [01.7](../lessons/module-01/lesson-07.md): dry oven gloves, a metal tin for steam (never glass), and step back when you pour the water.

## Steps

### Part A — the bakery order (about 60 minutes)

1. **Products and deadlines.** List each product, quantity, piece weight and deadline. Underline the earliest deadline.
2. **Dough and flour.** Calculate the dough for each product and the total, add 2% for losses, find the flour and round it up to the next 100 g.
3. **Technical sheet.** Fill in the production sheet template: every ingredient in % and in grams for the batch, and the total. Check it against the dough needed.
4. **Requisition (bon d'économat).** List what you take from the store and the fridge, with quantities and what you check (flour type, use-by date of the yeast, date on the pâte fermentée tub).
5. **Equipment.** List the equipment for each stage, from weighing to cooling. Check the mixer capacity against your dough total.
6. **Sequence and fermentation.** Write the stages in order with their times. Add up the time from end of mixing to the oven.
7. **Baking plan.** Decide which products go in which load and on which deck, and when each load comes out.
8. **Start time.** Work backwards from the earliest deadline to the latest time you can start weighing. Then check the other two deadlines against your oven plan.
9. **Problem spotting.** The bâtards and rolls cannot go in the oven until the first load comes out. Write what happens to their apprêt if all pieces are shaped at the same time, and what you would do about it.

### Part B — the home order (about 4 hours, of which 1 hour hands-on)

10. Repeat steps 1-8 for the home order, at home scale (no loss allowance; check that 500 g of flour is enough).
11. Do the mise en place and bake the order following [lesson 01.7](../lessons/module-01/lesson-07.md), but divide into 12 pieces of 70 g and shape them into round rolls (boulage, a little tighter than for pre-shaping). Bake 14-16 minutes.
12. Record everything in the bake log: planned and actual temperatures, times, piece weights, baked weight of one roll.
13. Cut one cooled roll and score it with the quality rubric.
14. Write three lines: what went to plan, what did not, what you will change next time.

## Deliverables

- Part A: a completed production sheet for the order, the requisition, the equipment list, the sequence with fermentation times, the oven plan, the start time and your answer to step 9.
- Part B: the same file for the home order, a completed bake log, a rubric score out of 20 and your three lines.
- Optional: a photo of your rolls and of one cut roll, kept with your log so you can compare with later bakes.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Quantities | Dough and flour correct; every ingredient from the rounded flour; batch ≥ order + losses | Percent-of-dough slip, forgotten product or loss allowance |
| Requisition | Every item with quantity and the check to make | Items missing or no checks |
| Equipment | Every stage covered, including cooling; mixer capacity checked | Stages missing |
| Sequence and times | All stages in order; total fermentation time added up | Stages out of order or times missing |
| Oven plan | Loads fit the decks; every deadline met with cooling time | A deadline missed or deck capacity exceeded |
| Problem spotting (step 9) | Over-proofing of the second load identified with a workable fix | Not identified |
| Home bake: control | Dough 23-25 °C (or the difference explained); 12 pieces at 70 g ±2 g | Temperatures not measured; pieces not weighed |
| Home bake: result | Rolls risen, golden top and bottom, crumb baked through; rubric completed honestly | No rubric or no log |
| Hygiene and safety | Station set up and cleaned; hands washed at the right moments; safe steaming | Steps skipped |

The Academy cannot grade your bread or your photos. Compare your work honestly with this rubric and the reference answers below; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.
