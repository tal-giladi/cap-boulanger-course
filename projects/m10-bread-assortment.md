# Project: Bread Assortment

A bakery shelf is more than baguettes: from one pain courant dough come épis, shaped pieces and rolls, and beside it stand the "other breads", each with its own dough, timing, oven and salt limit. In Part A you prepare the paperwork for a Saturday order of that kind: three batches, their water temperatures, a levain build, salt checks, an oven plan and the allergen lines for the labels. In Part B you bake an assortment at home over two days: an épi, two shaped pieces and rolls in three shapes from one pain courant dough, plus one other bread of your choice. The Academy cannot grade your bread or your photos: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference answers; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Commande de production, samedi (magasin 7:00)**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Poids | Fiche |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Épi | 6 | pâton 350 g | PC-02 |
> | Pain fendu | 8 | pâton 400 g | PC-02 |
> | Couronne | 4 | pâton 500 g | PC-02 |
> | Petits pains (12 ronds, 12 longs, 12 tabatières) | 36 | pâton 60 g | PC-02 |
> | Pain de campagne (boule) | 8 | **poids cuit 500 g** | CA-01 |
> | Pain de mie (moule 30 × 10 × 10 cm, couvercle) | 4 | 0,35 g de pâte par cm³ | PM-01 |
>
> PC-02: farine T55 100 %, eau 64 %, sel 1,8 %, levure fraîche 1,5 %, pâte fermentée 15 % (total 182,3 %).
> CA-01 (formule globale): T65 85 %, seigle T130 15 %, eau 70 %, sel 1,8 % (total 171,8 %); 20 % de la farine en levain liquide (100 %) à la T65; perte à la cuisson 15 %; levain de tout point: ensemencement 20 %, marge 60 g.
> PM-01: farine T55 100 %, eau 60 %, lait en poudre 4 %, sel 1,6 %, levure fraîche 3,5 %, sucre 5 %, beurre 8 % (total 182,1 %).
> Pertes de fabrication 2 %; farine arrondie à la centaine de grammes supérieure; pâtons arrondis aux 5 g supérieurs.
> Relevés: farine 21 °C, fournil 23 °C, pâte fermentée 6 °C, levain 26 °C. TPV: PC-02 24 °C, CA-01 24 °C, PM-01 25 °C. Facteurs de friction: PC-02 26 (4 facteurs), CA-01 16 (4 facteurs), PM-01 24 (3 facteurs).
> Pertes à la cuisson mesurées: fendu 18 %, petit pain 23 %, pain de mie 10 %.
> Four: 3 soles avec buée et un four ventilé.

In English: one PC-02 dough for épis, fendus, couronnes and rolls in three shapes; 8 campagne boules of 500 g baked weight on liquid levain; 4 lidded pains de mie. Formulas, rounding rules, temperature readings, friction factors, measured baking losses and the ovens are given.

## You need

- Part A: calculator, three copies of the [production sheet template](../templates/production-sheet.md), the [organigramme template](../templates/organigramme.md), the [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md).
- Part B: the home equipment of lessons [10.1](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md) and [10.2](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md) (scales, probe thermometer, bowl, scraper, lidded containers, rolling pin or thin rod, floured tea towels as a couche, two trays, metal steam tray, lame, sharp scissors, oven gloves, wire rack), plus what your chosen other bread needs (lessons [10.3](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md) to [10.7](../lessons/module-10/lesson-07.md)); the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md) and the [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md).
- Time for Part B: an evening (pâte fermentée, and the levain build or soaker if your other bread needs it) and a long day, about 3 hours of it hands-on.

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses a 240-250 °C oven, steam and blades. Dry oven gloves and long sleeves; steam only in a metal tray preheated on the lowest shelf, never water into a hot glass dish; pour and step back. Cut the épi with the scissors pointing away from your other hand; score with the blade moving away from your fingers; cover the lame and close the scissors after use. If your other bread is a viennois, egg wash is raw egg: keep it cold and discard the rest.

## Steps

### Part A — the Saturday order (about 90 minutes)

1. **PC-02.** Dough requirement for the four PC-02 products; batch with 2 %; flour (rounded up) and every ingredient; check the total.
2. **CA-01.** Pâton from the baked weight; dough; overall batch; levain and final dough; the levain de tout point to build (flour, water, seed with the 60 g margin); the most fresh yeast the chef could add and still sell it "au levain".
3. **PM-01.** Pâton for the tin; batch; every ingredient.
4. **Water temperatures** for the three doughs.
5. **Salt checks** (g per 100 g of bread) for a fendu, a roll, a campagne boule and a pain de mie, each against its own limit.
6. **Oven plan.** Which product goes on which oven, at what temperature, with or without steam, in which order, so everything is cooled for 7:00. Explain any product baked without steam.
7. **Allergen lines** for the labels of the PC-02 products, the campagne and the pain de mie.
8. **Fill in** one production sheet per dough and one organigramme.

### Part B — your home assortment (two days)

9. **Evening:** make the pâte fermentée for a 1 kg PC-02 batch (lesson [08.1](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md): 100 g flour, 64 g water, 1.8 g salt, 1.5 g fresh yeast), and, if needed, the levain build ([10.3](../lessons/module-10/lesson-03.md)) or the soaker ([10.7](../lessons/module-10/lesson-07.md)) for your other bread.
10. **Plan on paper** before you start: PC-02 on 1 kg of flour (1,823 g, keep 150 g as your next pâte fermentée); divide **1 épi of 270 g, 1 fendu of 400 g, 1 couronne or tabatière of 400 g, 9 rolls of 60 g in three shapes** (1,610 g). Choose your other bread (campagne, complet, pain de mie or viennois) on 500 g of flour. Draw a timeline with both doughs and a one-tray oven: what bakes first, at what temperature, with or without steam.
11. **Mix** PC-02 by hand to 24 °C ± 1 °C (four factors, lesson [08.1](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)) and your other dough to its target, recording every temperature.
12. **Divide, shape and proof** following lessons [10.1](../lessons/module-10/lesson-01.md) and [10.2](../lessons/module-10/lesson-02.md): fendu groove down, tabatière flap down, couronne with its hole open, rolls rounded with tension; the épi cut with scissors at the end of apprêt.
13. **Bake** in the order of your timeline; steam for the PC-02 products and the free-form campagne or complet, none for pain de mie or egg-washed viennois.
14. **Evaluate** after cooling: weigh every piece, calculate the losses and the salt per 100 g of the lightest roll and of your other bread against its own limit, and score the épi, one shaped piece, the roll tray and the other bread with the quality rubric.
15. **Write three lines:** what matched your plan, what did not, the one change for next time.

## Deliverables

- Part A: the calculations for steps 1-7, three production sheets, one organigramme and the oven plan.
- Part B: the timeline, the temperature log, the bake log with all times and weights, the losses and salt checks, four rubric scores and your three lines.
- Optional: a photo of the whole assortment and of one cut section of each bread, kept with your log for your own comparison.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| PC-02 batch | All four products in the requirement; 2 % added; flour rounded up; total ≥ need | A product forgotten; flour rounded down |
| CA-01 and levain | Pâton = baked weight ÷ 0.85, rounded up; levain and final dough add up; build with margin; yeast limit on final-mixing flour | Loss added as × 1.15; levain water added on top; 0.2 % of all flour |
| PM-01 | Pâton from the tin volume; batch correct | Pâton guessed or copied from another tin |
| Water temperatures | Four factors for PC-02 and CA-01 (pâte fermentée, levain), three for PM-01 | Pre-ferment left out; three and four factors mixed |
| Salt checks | Each product with its own loss and its own limit (1.4 / 1.3 / 1.1 g) | One limit for all; % of dough reported as g per 100 g |
| Oven plan | Fast products and long bakes separated; pain de mie without steam at a lower temperature; everything cooled by 7:00 | Pain de mie with steam at 250 °C; cooling forgotten |
| Home shaping | Épi with alternating leaves still joined; fendu and tabatière opened as designed; couronne hole open; rolls alike within each shape | Leaves torn off; groove or lid closed; rolls of different sizes |
| Home baking and control | Each product baked to colour and core temperature; every piece weighed after cooling; salt checked; four rubrics completed | Pale or burnt products; weights or rubric missing |
| Safety and hygiene | Oven, steam, lame and scissors handled as in the warning; allergens named; station cleaned | Steps skipped |
