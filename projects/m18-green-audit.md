# Project: Green Audit

An environmental audit is how a bakery turns "we should waste less" into numbers, decisions and a plan someone can follow. In Part A you audit one week of a small bakery from its oven log, meter readings, waste log, packaging list and cleaning-product sheet, calculate what each problem costs and write the action plan. In Part B you run the same audit on your own kitchen for a week, using the work you did in lessons 18.1 to 18.3, and measure the difference in a second week. The Academy cannot grade your audit: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference answers; this self-assessment does not replace the official exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Audit environnemental, semaine du mardi 6 au dimanche 11 octobre**
>
> **Four:** four à soles électrique, 3 étages de 5 m². Mise en route à 2:00; premier enfournement 5:30; temps de chauffe réel (carte du four) 1 h 30. Générateur de buée en marche sur les 3 étages toute la matinée. Pains de campagne cuits à 250 °C (un essai à 230 °C la semaine dernière était conforme à la grille qualité). L'étage 3 cuit la viennoiserie à 190 °C de 9:30 à 10:00 puis reste allumé jusqu'à 13:00.
>
> **Froid:** chambre froide positive, consigne −1 °C (fiche maison: 0 à 4 °C). Test de la feuille de papier: elle glisse sans résistance. Condenseur poussiéreux; groupe installé contre le mur du four.
>
> **Eau:** robinet de la plonge 12 L/min, ouvert environ 1 h 30 par jour. Compteur samedi 20:00: 1 245,300 m³; dimanche 6:00, boutique fermée, rien utilisé: 1 245,330 m³. Prix de l'eau: 4,20 € le m³.
>
> **Électricité:** 0,20 € le kWh.
>
> **Déchets (semaine):** invendus 150 baguettes (dont 120 de la fournée de 16:00), 36 traditions, 40 croissants. Dans la poubelle d'ordures ménagères: chutes de pâte, pain moisi, coquilles d'œufs, sacs de farine et cartons. Volume total de déchets environ 600 L par semaine; collecte municipale.
>
> **Coûts de revient (exemple):** baguette 0,60 €, tradition 0,70 €, croissant 0,35 €.
>
> **Emballages et comptoir:** sacs plastique 15 µm sans marquage pour brioches et pains au lait; sachets papier à fenêtre plastique pour les baguettes; couverts plastique jetables au coin snack (18 places assises); tickets de caisse imprimés à chaque vente.
>
> **Produits d'entretien:** détergent sols "20 mL par litre", seau de 10 L, dose versée mesurée à environ 500 mL; eau de javel et détartrant acide rangés ensemble et utilisés dans le même évier; aucun produit écolabellisé.

In English: one week of the bakery's oven habits, cold room, water meter, waste, packaging and cleaning products, with prices and example production costs. The bakery is a small craft bakery that uses the municipal waste collection.

## You need

- Part A: calculator; lessons [18.1](../lessons/module-18/lesson-01.md), [18.2](../lessons/module-18/lesson-02.md) and [18.3](../lessons/module-18/lesson-03.md); the [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md) for the two most urgent findings; paper or a spreadsheet for the action plan.
- Part B: your oven card and bake-day energy plan (18.1), your one-week waste log and reuse plan (18.2), your packaging sheet and dose card (18.3); kitchen scale, 1 L jug, measuring cup, your electricity and water bills; the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md) for the bake day of week two.
- Time: Part A about 2 hours; Part B two weeks of logging (5-10 minutes a day) plus one bake day and a 1-hour write-up.

> [!WARNING]
> Part B includes a bake day with a 230-250 °C oven and steam (dry gloves, preheated metal tray, face turned away), pain perdu with raw egg (wash hands and tools after) and cleaning products (gloves, never mix two products, never decant into a food container). Read each lesson's safety box before you start.

## Steps

### Part A — the bakery's audit

1. **Oven.** Calculate the empty running time and its yearly cost with the CNBPF figure; give the right switch-on time. Decide the baking temperature of the campagne, the steam on each deck, and when deck 3 should be switched off.
2. **Cold.** Give the correct set point, what the paper test means, and the two actions on the condenser and the unit's position (who does them).
3. **Water.** Calculate the plonge's daily use and what an aerator at 6-8 L/min would save per day and per year (300 days) in m³ and euros. Interpret the overnight meter readings: flow per hour, yearly volume, cost, likely source.
4. **Waste.** Calculate the weekly and yearly (50 weeks) cost of the unsold products. Write the plan by level of the hierarchy, from prevention down. Say which sorting obligations apply to this bakery and what goes in which bin.
5. **Packaging and counter.** For each item, say whether it is allowed, and what replaces it if not. Say whether the snack corner must use reusable tableware.
6. **Cleaning products.** Calculate the right dose, the waste per bucket and per year (2 buckets a day, 300 days). State the storage and use rule for bleach and acid, and what to look for at the next order.
7. **Action plan.** One table: action, figure (kWh, m³, €, kg), who decides (the employee within the house sheet, or the owner), deadline. Rank by saving and by ease.
8. **Report.** Fill in a non-conformity report for the two findings that could hurt someone or break a rule.

### Part B — your kitchen's audit

9. **Week one, baseline.** For seven days log: bread and baking waste by weight, reason and destination (18.2's log); packaging from baking ingredients by stream; the oven-on time of every bake; your tap flow and overnight meter test (18.1); your usual cleaning dose (18.3).
10. **Your plan.** Write three to five actions, each with a figure: for example one preheat a week instead of three, half-size loaves on weekdays, chapelure every Friday, dose card on the bucket, cloth bag for bought bread.
11. **Week two.** Apply the plan. Include one bake day run on a single preheat with a product on the falling heat (18.1) and one reuse bake of stale bread (18.2). Log the same items.
12. **Compare** week one and week two: kg of bread wasted, oven-on hours and estimated kWh and cost, packaging items, mL of cleaning product.
13. **Write one page:** results table, what worked, what did not and why, what you keep.

## Deliverables

- Part A: calculations for steps 1, 3, 4 and 6; the decisions for steps 2 and 5; the ranked action plan; two non-conformity reports.
- Part B: the two weekly logs, your plan, the bake log of the single-preheat day, the comparison table and your one-page conclusion.

1. **Oven.** Real preheat 1 h 30 before 5:30 → switch on at **4:00**; the oven now runs empty about 2 hours. CNBPF: about **12 kWh a day**, over **3,300 kWh a year**; × 0.20 € ≈ **660 € a year**. Campagne: the 230 °C test conformed, so bake at **230 °C** (about 10 % less for that deck). Steam: on the bread decks at loading only; **off on deck 3** for viennoiserie (steam can be up to 15 % of a bread bake's energy). Deck 3: switch off about 10 minutes before the end of the 9:30-10:00 bake, not at 13:00; regroup any late bake onto a deck still hot.
2. **Cold.** Set to about **+3 °C**: the house range is 0-4 °C, and every degree colder than needed costs more than 5 %. A sheet that slides out freely means a **worn seal**: report it for replacement. Dusty condenser and a unit against the oven wall (a unit in a hot room uses far more, about 40 % in a 30 °C room against 18 °C): the owner has the condenser cleaned and the unit moved or shielded by the technician.
3. **Water.** Plonge: 12 L/min × 90 min = **1,080 L a day**. With an aerator at 6-8 L/min: 540-720 L, so **360-540 L a day** saved; × 300 days = **108-162 m³ a year**; × 4.20 € ≈ **454-680 € a year**, plus the energy to heat that water if it is hot. Overnight: 1,245.330 − 1,245.300 = 0.030 m³ = **30 L in 10 h = 3 L/h**, about 26 m³ a year (the CCI table's light toilet-cistern leak, about 30 m³ a year), ≈ **110-126 € a year**. Find it (toilet, dripping tap, steam generator feed) and have it repaired.
4. **Waste.** Week: 150 × 0.60 + 36 × 0.70 + 40 × 0.35 = 90 + 25.20 + 14 = **129.20 €**; × 50 weeks ≈ **6,460 € a year**. Plan: (1) prevent: reduce the 16:00 load (120 of the 150 unsold come from it), add a small later load if the shelf empties, croissants baked in smaller batches through the morning; (2) use for people: anti-waste basket in the evening, plain baguettes into chapelure and croûtons and up to about 5 % dry crumb in pain courant (never in tradition), donation of the edible remainder to an association (60 % tax reduction on cost); (3) animal feed only through an allowed channel; (4) biowaste bin for dough scraps, spoiled bread and eggshells, **compulsory for every professional since 1 January 2024**; mouldy bread never reused. Sorting: flour sacks (shaken out) and cardboard flattened into the paper stream; film and plastic as the commune says. At about 600 L a week on the municipal collection (under 1,100 L), the bakery follows the **commune's sorting rules** rather than contracting separate collection for each of the 6 streams, but it still sorts.
5. **Packaging and counter.** 15 µm bags without home-compostable and biosourced marks: **not allowed** (under 50 µm only home-compostable, at least 60 % biosourced, NF T51-800); replace with paper bags for same-day sale or allowed bags or boxes for products kept longer. Paper bags with a plastic window: allowed but mixed material; prefer all-paper bags when restocking. Plastic cutlery: **banned**; wooden or reusable cutlery, on request. Snack corner of 18 seats: under 20 people served at once, so reusable tableware is not compulsory, but it is the better choice. Receipts: printed **only on request**.
6. **Cleaning products.** 20 mL × 10 L = **200 mL** per bucket; 500 − 200 = **300 mL wasted** per bucket; × 2 × 300 = **180 L a year**. Dosing cup marked at 200 mL or a dilution station. Bleach and acid descaler **never in the same sink** and stored apart: together they give off chlorine (INRS). Next order: compare a detergent with the EU Ecolabel or NF Environnement for the floor; disinfection products stay those of the hygiene plan.
7. **Action plan (example ranking).** Free and immediate: preheat at 4:00 (660 €), steam off on deck 3, deck 3 off at 10:00, campagne at 230 °C, cold room to +3 °C, dose cup, separate bleach and acid, receipts on request, biowaste bin, 16:00 load reduced. Small cost: aerator, seal, leak repair, new bags and cutlery. Owner and technician: condenser and unit position.
8. **Non-conformity reports.** The two most urgent: bleach and acid used in the same sink (risk of toxic gas); banned plastic bags and cutlery at the counter (rule broken, fine). The biowaste in the residual bin is a close third.
