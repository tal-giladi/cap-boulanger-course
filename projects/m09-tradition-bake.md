# Project: Tradition Bake

Your first pain de tradition française from the legal check to the tasting. In Part A you prepare a bakery order of tradition: check a new flour against the decree, scale the TR-01 batch including tomorrow's tradition pâte fermentée, calculate the water, check the salt, build the timeline and answer the owner's freezing idea. In Part B you make a 1 kg batch of TR-01 at home with an autolyse, bassinage and folds, shape six baguettes, bake them in two loads, evaluate them as a batch and compare them with your pain courant from [Module 8](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md). The Academy cannot grade your bread or your photos: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference answers; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Commande de production, samedi**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Poids pâton | Pour |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Baguette de tradition (TR-01) | 50 | 300 g | magasin 7:30 |
> | Petit pain de tradition (TR-01) | 16 | 80 g | livraison restaurant 8:00 |
>
> Fiche TR-01: farine de tradition 100 %, eau 70 % (68 % à l'autolyse + 2 % de bassinage), sel 1,8 %, levure fraîche 0,6 %, pâte fermentée de tradition 15 %. TPV 23 °C. Pertes de fabrication 2 %; farine arrondie à la centaine de grammes supérieure.
> **Garder 1 200 g de pâte fermentée de tradition pour dimanche**, prise sur ce lot. Pâte fermentée de tradition en stock: 1 500 g.
> Relevés à 1:00: farine 21 °C, fournil 23 °C, pâte fermentée 5 °C. Facteur de friction TR-01 (4 facteurs, autolyse + pétrissage lent): 14.
> Temps: pesée 15 min, autolyse 30 min, pétrissage 15 min, pointage 2 h (rabats à 40 et 80 min), division et préfaçonnage 20 min, détente 30 min, façonnage 30 min, apprêt 1 h, cuisson baguettes 25 min (four 4 soles, 13 baguettes par sole), petits pains 15 min, ressuage 30 min minimum.
> Pertes à la cuisson mesurées: baguettes 22 %, petits pains 24 %.
>
> **Nouveau moulin — fiche technique:** « Farine de tradition française T65: farine de blé, farine de fèves 1,0 %, farine de malt de blé 0,3 %, gluten de blé. »
>
> **Mot du patron:** « Pour lundi, on pourrait façonner dimanche et congeler les traditions crues? »

In English: 50 tradition baguettes at 300 g for the shop at 7:30 and 16 tradition rolls at 80 g for an 8:00 delivery, TR-01. The batch must also provide Sunday's 1,200 g of tradition pâte fermentée. Readings, friction factor, stage times and baking losses are given. A new mill's specification sheet, and the owner's idea of freezing shaped traditions on Sunday for Monday.

## You need

- Part A: calculator, the [production sheet template](../templates/production-sheet.md), the [organigramme template](../templates/organigramme.md) and the [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md).
- Part B: the home equipment of lessons [09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md) to [09.4](../lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md) (scales, probe thermometer, large bowl, scraper, lidded containers, couche or linen tea towel, rice flour for dusting, two trays or a stone, metal steam tray, lame, oven gloves, wire rack, bread knife, paper bags), additive-free flour (see [Flour in Israel](../references/flour-in-israel.md)), the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md), the [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md) and the [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md).
- Your [Module 8](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md) bake log and rubric score for pain courant, for the comparison.
- Time for Part B: an evening (15 minutes) and about 6 hours the next day, of which about 1 h 30 hands-on; 15 minutes the morning after for the keeping test.

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses a 250 °C oven and steam. Dry oven gloves and long sleeves; steam only in a metal tray preheated in the oven, never water into a hot glass dish; pour and step back; fan off and steam out at about 10 minutes. Score with the blade moving away from your fingers, keep the guard on the lame between uses, and cut bread with a serrated knife on a stable board.

## Steps

### Part A — the bakery order (about 60 minutes)

1. **Flour check.** Can the new mill's flour be used for pain de tradition française? Name the article and say what, if anything, may still be added at the bench.
2. **Dough requirement.** Baguettes and rolls, then the total including Sunday's 1,200 g of tradition pâte fermentée.
3. **Batch.** Add 2 % process losses, calculate the flour (round up to the next 100 g) and every ingredient, splitting the water into autolyse water and eau de réserve. Check the stock of pâte fermentée covers 15 %. Check the batch total covers the requirement.
4. **Water temperature** with the pâte fermentée as fourth factor.
5. **Salt check.** Salt per 100 g of bread for a baguette and a roll with the measured losses.
6. **Timeline.** Work backwards from 7:30: when must weighing start, when are the baguettes in the oven, and when are the rolls out and cooled?
7. **The owner's idea.** Answer him in two or three sentences: is it allowed, and what do you propose instead?
8. **Fill in** one production sheet (TR-01 for this batch, with temperatures and process) and one organigramme.

### Part B — the home batch (about 6 hours)

9. **The evening before:** make about 173 g of tradition pâte fermentée with your additive-free flour (flour 100 g, water 70 g, salt 1.8 g, fresh yeast 1.5 g or 0.5 g instant); 1 hour at room temperature (20 minutes in a hot summer kitchen), then the fridge.
10. **Plan:** TR-01 on 1 kg of flour: flour 1,000 g, water 680 g in the autolyse + 20 g eau de réserve, salt 18 g, fresh yeast 6 g (or 2 g instant), pâte fermentée 150 g; total 1,874 g. Six pâtons of 270 g (1,620 g) and 150 g kept as your next tradition pâte fermentée. With Israeli white flour, be ready to go up to 72-74 % water by bassinage and record it.
11. **Mix** by hand to 23 °C ± 1 °C with the four-factor water calculation and an autolyse of 30 minutes (lesson [09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)). Record every temperature and the final hydration.
12. **Pointage** about 2 hours with folds at 30, 60 and 90 minutes, ended on the signs; keep 150 g of pâte fermentée, labelled "TR".
13. **Divide** 6 × 270 g ± 2 g, pre-shape loosely, détente about 30 minutes (lesson [09.3](../lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)).
14. **Shape** set 1 (three baguettes), then set 2 about 25 minutes later, with light hands and sealed seams; measure every baguette; floured couche.
15. **Score and bake** each set when the poke test says ready: three overlapping cuts, steam, 240-250 °C, 22-27 minutes, deep golden-brown, core at least 93 °C (lesson [09.4](../lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)).
16. **Evaluate** as a batch control after 1 hour of cooling: weights, average, loss, salt per 100 g with 1.044 %, rubric for one baguette of each set, the main defect traced to its stage, a three-line report.
17. **Compare** with your [Module 8](../lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md) pain courant on the eight lines of the comparison table in lesson 09.4.
18. **Next morning:** taste the tradition you kept in a paper bag (and a pain courant if you have one). Write three lines: what matched the plan, what did not, the one change for your next tradition.

## Deliverables

- Part A: the flour decision with its article, the calculations for steps 2-6, the completed production sheet and organigramme, and the note to the owner.
- Part B: the temperature log (inputs, calculated water, dough temperature, final hydration), the bake log with every time and weight (pâtons, lengths, cooled weights), loss and salt per 100 g, two rubric scores, the defect analysis, the report, the comparison with pain courant and the next-morning note.
- Optional: a photo of the six baguettes and a cut section next to a pain courant, kept with your log for your own comparison.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Legal check | Flour rejected for tradition because of the added gluten (art. 2); malt already at 0.3 % noted; freezing idea refused with art. 2 and L122-17 and a retarding alternative | Flour accepted because bean and malt are within limits; freezing allowed "because it is baked here" |
| Batch calculation | Sunday's pâte fermentée counted in the requirement; 2 % added; flour rounded up; water split 68 % + 2 %; every ingredient correct; total ≥ requirement | Pâte fermentée taken from the order's dough; flour rounded down; reserve water forgotten |
| Water temperature | Four factors, all four inputs, four-factor friction factor for this method | Three factors, or the pâte fermentée left out |
| Salt check | Both products checked with their own measured loss and 1.044 % | 1.8 % used as if it were the salt in the bread |
| Timeline | Worked back from 7:30 including autolyse, baking and cooling; start within 10 minutes of the reference | Autolyse or cooling forgotten |
| Home dough | Additive-free flour for dough and pâte fermentée; 22-24 °C after mixing; bassinage recorded; three folds; pointage ended on the signs | Flour not checked; temperature not measured; pointage by the clock only |
| Shaping | Six pâtons within ± 2 g; gas kept; seams closed; lengths within ± 2 cm | Dough punched; seams open; weights or lengths not recorded |
| Baking | Apprêt ended on the poke test; steam safely made; deep golden-brown; core ≥ 93 °C | Pale bread; no steam; core not checked |
| Evaluation and comparison | Sample weighed after cooling; loss and salt calculated; rubric complete; defect traced to a stage with evidence; eight-line comparison and next-morning note | Bread judged warm; comparison by memory only |
| Safety and hygiene | Oven, steam and blade handled as in the warning; station cleaned | Steps skipped |
