# Project: Role-Play Scripts

A baker's day is full of short conversations that decide money and safety: the delivery driver waiting for a signature, a machine that must be stopped, a seller who needs the new product explained, a customer asking about sesame. In this project you write the scripts for six such scenes from one Saturday morning, in French with the English meaning, produce the documents that go with them, then play each scene aloud with a partner and record it. The Academy cannot hear your recordings or grade your French: compare your scripts and recordings honestly with the rubric and the model scene, and remember that this self-assessment does not replace the official exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — samedi 17 octobre, équipe du matin**
>
> **5:50 — Réception.** Livraison crèmerie. Bon de livraison: « Beurre de tourage 82 % 2 × 5 kg ; crème UHT 35 % 6 × 1 L ; œufs 2 plateaux de 30 ». Relevés: beurre 4 °C ; crème UHT température ambiante (produit stable, conforme) ; un plateau d'œufs avec 4 œufs cassés. Le livreur est pressé.
>
> **6:20 — Fournil.** Le pétrin à spirale s'arrête net en 2e vitesse pendant le pétrissage des baguettes (pâte PC-02 pour 24 baguettes) ; odeur de chaud au moteur. Le chef est au four.
>
> **6:40 — Défournement.** Fournée 2 de campagnes au levain (12 pièces) : 3 pièces au pied brûlé, sole du bas.
>
> **6:50 — Passation au magasin.** Nouveau produit du jour : pain aux noix (T65, farine de seigle 10 %, cerneaux de noix 20 %, levain, sans levure ajoutée ; analyse de laboratoire pH 4,2, acide acétique 1 000 ppm). Fiche d'analyse sensorielle : croûte grillée 7, noix 8, acide 3, mie moelleuse. Tableau des allergènes : gluten (blé, seigle), fruits à coque (noix) ; traces possibles de sésame (même plan de travail que le pain aux graines).
>
> **9:30 — Comptoir.** Une cliente : « Mon mari est allergique aux fruits à coque et moi je suis cœliaque. Qu'est-ce qu'on peut prendre pour un plateau de fromages ce soir ? Et comment je garde le pain jusqu'à ce soir ? »
>
> **12:30 — Fin de service.** Passation à la boulangère de l'après-midi : le pétrin est hors service, technicien prévu à 14 h ; pâte de tradition en pointage retardé, chambre froide, bacs 5 et 6, sortie dimanche 4 h ; levain rafraîchi à 12 h.

In English: one Saturday morning in six scenes: a dairy delivery with broken eggs, a spiral mixer that stops with a hot smell, three campagnes with burnt bottoms, a new walnut bread (with lab report, tasting results and allergens) to brief to the shop, a customer whose husband has a nut allergy and who is coeliac herself, and the hand-over to the afternoon baker.

## You need

- Lessons [19.1](../lessons/module-19/lesson-01.md) to [19.4](../lessons/module-19/lesson-04.md) and their phrase tables; [09.1](../lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md) for the bread names; [07.5](../lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md) for storage.
- Templates: [delivery check](../templates/delivery-check.md), [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md) (×3).
- A partner (family member, friend, conversation partner) to play the chef, the driver, the seller and the customer; if they do not speak French, give them their lines in French to read, or play both parts yourself.
- A phone to record audio; paper or a document for the scripts.
- Time: about 3 hours: 90 minutes writing, 60 minutes rehearsing and recording, 30 minutes self-evaluation.

## Steps

1. **Read the brief** and list, for each scene, who speaks to whom, the register (tu or vous, fournil or plain words) and the document that goes with it.
2. **Scene 1, reception (C4.1).** Fill in the delivery check. Write the script: your report to the chef (facts, what you did, proposal, question) and the reservation sentence for the delivery note. Decide what happens to the 4 broken eggs and the rest of the tray.
3. **Scene 2, mixer (C4.4).** Write the script: what you do before speaking (emergency stop, tag), your 20-second report to the chef, the proposal for the dough in the bowl. Fill in a non-conformity report (type: equipment).
4. **Scene 3, burnt bottoms (C4.4).** Write the report to the chef with the count, the deck and a probable cause to check; fill in a non-conformity report (type: production). Write the one line for the seller about the 3 set-aside campagnes.
5. **Scene 4, new product brief (C4.2).** Check the name first: may it be "pain aux noix au levain"? Write the pitch card (seven blocks) and the two-minute brief, including the seller repeating it back and one correction.
6. **Scene 5, customer (C2.6, C4.2).** Write the conversation: show the table, answer for the husband's nut allergy and the customer's coeliac disease honestly, suggest what (if anything) the table clears, give storage advice for tonight and one pairing.
7. **Scene 6, hand-over (C4.3).** Write a 45-second brief for the afternoon baker.
8. **Every script** in two columns: French line, then English meaning. Mark each fournil term you used and check it against the [glossary](../glossary.md).
9. **Rehearse** each scene twice with your partner, then record it. Time each one.
10. **Self-evaluate** with the rubric, listen back, rewrite the weakest scene and record it again.

## Deliverables

- Six scripts in French with English meaning, each with its speaker, register and timing.
- The delivery check and three non-conformity reports (delivery, mixer, campagnes).
- The pitch card for the pain aux noix.
- Six recordings (or one recording of the six scenes) and one re-recorded scene.
- A half-page self-evaluation: scores from the rubric, the three words or phrases you hesitated over, what you changed in the re-recording.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Reports to the chef (scenes 1-3) | Facts with numbers first, then what you did, a proposal and a question for the decision; each under 30 seconds | Story before facts; no number; no proposal; blame |
| Delivery reservation | Product, quantity, defect and time, written before signing; broken eggs refused, rest checked | "Non conforme" with no detail; signed first |
| Machine safety | Emergency stop, "hors service" tag, nothing opened or repaired, chef told at once | Restarted to "try again"; motor cover opened |
| Pitch card and brief | Seven blocks from the documents; name checked against the lab report; seller's sentence under 30 words; repeat-back done | Adjectives instead of facts; "au levain" not checked; no allergen line |
| Customer conversation | Table shown; nuts and traces stated; nothing offered as gluten-free; storage and pairing correct | Guessing ("normalement"); spelt or rye offered; fridge advised |
| Hand-over | Done, in progress with times, where things wait, what is broken; under 45 seconds | Missing times or places; the broken mixer not mentioned |
| Register and vocabulary | Vous to chef, driver and customer; fournil words only in the fournil; correct articles | Tu to customers; jargon at the counter; article errors |

**Scene 1, model script**

| French | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Chef, j'ai un problème sur la livraison d'œufs : quatre œufs cassés sur un plateau. | Chef, I have a problem with the egg delivery: four broken eggs on one tray. |
| Le beurre est à quatre degrés, la crème UHT est conforme. | The butter is at 4 °C, the UHT cream conforms. |
| J'ai mis le plateau de côté. Le livreur attend. | I've set the tray aside. The driver is waiting. |
| Je propose de refuser le plateau abîmé et de faire une réserve. Vous êtes d'accord ? | I suggest refusing the damaged tray and writing a reservation. Do you agree? |

Reservation: « Œufs : 1 plateau de 30 refusé, 4 œufs cassés ; 5 h 52. » The tray with broken eggs is refused as damaged goods (the reception rule of lesson 19.1: damaged packaging is refused); the second tray is checked egg by egg before it is accepted.

**Key decisions for the other scenes**

- **Scene 2:** emergency stop, tag "hors service", no restart, nothing opened; report: « Le pétrin s'est arrêté net en deuxième vitesse, odeur de chaud au moteur. Je l'ai arrêté et étiqueté. La pâte des baguettes est dans la cuve : je propose de la finir à la main ou au batteur si vous voulez. » The chef decides; the next mixes are reorganised.
- **Scene 3:** « Fournée deux, trois campagnes sur douze au pied brûlé, toutes sur la sole du bas. Je les ai mises de côté. On vérifie la température de la sole du bas ? » Seller: « Trois campagnes ne vont pas en vente. »
- **Scene 4:** made and baked on site (décret 93-1074 art. 1), pH 4.2 ≤ 4.3 and 1,000 ≥ 900 ppm with no added yeast: "au levain" is allowed (art. 3). Seller's sentence, for example: « Notre nouveau pain aux noix au levain, avec un peu de seigle : croûte grillée, beaucoup de noix. Parfait avec le fromage. » Allergen line: gluten (blé, seigle), noix; traces de sésame.
- **Scene 5:** nothing is gluten-free: « Tous nos pains contiennent du gluten ; pour vous, je ne peux rien vous conseiller sans risque. » For the husband: not the pain aux noix; campagne or tradition if the table shows no nuts and no nut traces. Storage: paper bag, not the fridge, a few minutes in a hot oven before serving.
- **Scene 6:** « Pour la relève : pétrin hors service, le technicien passe à 14 h. Tradition en pointage retardé, chambre froide, bacs 5 et 6, sortie demain 4 h. Levain rafraîchi à midi. »
