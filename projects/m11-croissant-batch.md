# Project: Croissant Batch

A croissant order is planned a day ahead: the détrempe, the lamination and the shaping happen the afternoon before, and the cold holds the croissants until the oven. In Part A you prepare the paperwork for a Sunday croissant order: the batch, the water and ice, the butter plaque, the mini-croissant triangles, a two-day organigramme with a programmed proof, and the label. In Part B you make a full home batch of CR-01 over two days, from détrempe to evaluated croissants, with every temperature and time on record. The Academy cannot grade your croissants or your photos: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference answers; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Commande de production, dimanche (magasin 7:00)**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Pâton | Fiche |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Croissant pur beurre | 96 | 60 g | CR-01 |
> | Mini-croissant pur beurre (hôtel, livraison 7:30) | 30 | 30 g | CR-01 |
>
> CR-01: farine T45 (gruau) 100 %, eau 26 %, lait entier 26 %, sucre 11 %, sel 2,0 %, levure fraîche 4,0 %, beurre (détrempe) 8 % (détrempe 177 %); beurre de tourage 50 % (pâte feuilletée 227 %). TPV détrempe 20 °C; 3 tours simples; abaisse environ 3,5 mm; triangles 11 × 30 cm pour 60 g.
> Chutes au détaillage: 10 % de la pâte feuilletée. Pertes de fabrication 2 %. Farine arrondie à la centaine de grammes supérieure.
> Relevés samedi 14:00: farine 20 °C, fournil 22 °C, lait (chambre froide) 4 °C, eau du robinet 20 °C. Pétrissage court au batteur à spirale: facteur de friction 12 (3 facteurs).
> Beurre de tourage 84 % MG en plaques de 2 kg. Une armoire de pousse contrôlée (blocage 3 °C, pousse 25 °C / 78 % HR). Four à soles.
> Équipe: samedi 14:00-19:00; dimanche à partir de 5:15.

In English: 96 croissants of 60 g and 30 mini-croissants of 30 g, all CR-01, made on Saturday afternoon and baked on Sunday morning. Trims, losses, rounding, temperature readings, friction factor, butter, proofer-retarder, oven and team hours are given.

## You need

- Part A: calculator, the [production sheet template](../templates/production-sheet.md), the [organigramme template](../templates/organigramme.md), the [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md).
- Part B: the home equipment of lessons [11.2](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md) to [11.6](../lessons/module-11/lesson-06.md) (scales, probe and oven thermometers, bowl, scraper, rolling pin, ruler, baking paper, cling film, a frozen tray or marble, pizza wheel or knife, pastry brush, two trays, oven gloves, wire rack, serrated knife); the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md), the [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md) and the [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md).
- Time for Part B: an evening (about 45 minutes, then overnight in the fridge) and a morning of about 6-7 hours, about 2 hours of it hands-on, or spread over three days with the shaped croissants proofed from the fridge.

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses a 190-200 °C oven, sharp blades and raw egg. Dry oven gloves; take trays out level (melted butter can spit). Cut with the blade moving away from your other hand and put the knife down flat. Egg wash: crack eggs into a separate bowl, keep the egg wash cold, discard the rest, wash your hands. Croissants contain wheat (gluten), milk and egg.

## Steps

### Part A — the Sunday order (about 90 minutes)

1. **Batch.** Dough in the pieces; laminated dough before cutting (trims); with losses; flour rounded up; every ingredient of the détrempe and the beurre de tourage; check the total.
2. **Water.** The liquid temperature, the water temperature (half the liquid is milk at 4 °C), and the ice and tap water for the water weight.
3. **Butter plaque.** One block for the whole batch: the plaque's side for a thickness of about 1.2 cm (butter about 0.91 g/cm³), the size of the détrempe square for an envelope lock-in, and how much of the 2 kg plaque is returned to the cold room.
4. **Mini-croissants.** Triangle dimensions that give 30 g from the same sheet, and why the same thickness is kept.
5. **Organigramme.** Saturday 14:00-19:00 and Sunday from 5:15: mixing, chilling, lock-in, three turns with rests, final rest, cutting and shaping, the proofer-retarder programme (hold and warm-up times), egg wash, baking order (minis and croissants), cooling, shop at 7:00 and hotel at 7:30.
6. **Label line** for the shop: name and allergens.
7. **Fill in** the production sheet and the organigramme.

### Part B — your home batch (two days)

8. **Day 1, evening:** read your kitchen survey (lesson [11.1](../lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md)). Calculate the water, mix CR-01 on 500 g of flour by hand to 18-22 °C (lesson [11.2](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md)), flatten, wrap, chill overnight. Start the temperature log.
9. **Day 2, cool window:** butter plaque of 250 g at about 13 °C, bend test, lock-in (lesson [11.3](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md)); three tours simples with 20-45-minute rests, turns marked (lesson [11.4](../lessons/module-11/lesson-04.md)); final rest at least 1 hour.
10. **Cut and shape** 16 croissants of about 60 g, the first triangle weighed and the thickness corrected; trims as twists (lesson [11.5](../lessons/module-11/lesson-05.md)).
11. **Proof** at 24-26 °C until almost doubled; bake one test croissant early (under-proof); egg-wash twice; bake without steam at 190-200 °C (fan 175-180 °C) until the creases are gold (lesson [11.6](../lessons/module-11/lesson-06.md)).
12. **Evaluate** after cooling (lesson [11.7](../lessons/module-11/lesson-07.md)): weights and baking loss, lengths, three cut sections, rubric out of 18, one non-conformity report or fault diagnosis.
13. **Write three lines:** what matched your plan, what did not, the one change for your next croissant batch. If you made the change from lesson [11.7](../lessons/module-11/lesson-07.md), say whether it worked.

## Deliverables

- Part A: the calculations for steps 1-4, the production sheet, the organigramme with the proofer-retarder programme, the label line.
- Part B: the temperature log (détrempe, butter, room at each turn, proof place, oven), the bake log with all times and weights, the rubric score, the cut-section descriptions, the report or diagnosis and your three lines.
- Optional: a photo of the tray and of one cut section, kept with your log for your own comparison with the next batch.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Batch calculation | Croissants and minis in the requirement; trims as ÷ 0.90; 2 % added; flour rounded up; beurre de tourage included; total ≥ need | Trims as × 1.10 or forgotten; détrempe only (÷ 1.77); flour rounded down |
| Water and ice | Three factors with friction; milk counted in the liquid; ice inside the water weight | Bread TPV used; milk ignored; ice added on top |
| Butter plaque | Side from volume ÷ thickness; détrempe about 1.5 × the butter side | Plaque size guessed |
| Mini-croissants | Half the triangle area at the same thickness | Same triangle cut thinner |
| Organigramme | Rests between turns, shaping on Saturday, programmed hold and warm-up, croissants and minis baked in time for 7:00 and 7:30 | Proof at room temperature overnight; no rests; oven times overlapping |
| Home détrempe and lamination | Détrempe 18-22 °C; butter 12-15 °C at the lock-in; three turns marked; no butter breaking or smearing (or corrected and recorded) | Temperatures not measured; turns not marked |
| Home cutting and shaping | 16 pieces within ± 3 g; tip underneath; same shape | Weights not checked; pieces of different sizes |
| Home proof and bake | Proof 24-26 °C until almost doubled; two thin coats; no steam; creases coloured; little or no butter on the paper | Warm proof; pool of butter; pale creases |
| Evaluation | Loss calculated; three cut sections described; rubric completed; fault traced to a stage with evidence | Impressions only; no link to the records |
| Safety and hygiene | Oven, blade and egg wash handled as in the warning; allergens named; station cleaned | Steps skipped |
