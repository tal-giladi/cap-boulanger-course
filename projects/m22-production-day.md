# Project: Production Day

The capstone of the course. In Part A you do a timed written phase on a new practice order: the technical sheet of a tradition dough and the organigramme of the whole order. In Part B you run a full production day at home, four doughs and a crème pâtissière, from your own plan, with an observer if you can. In Part C you present, score and report, then turn the readiness checklist into a dated plan for what you still need before the exam. The Academy cannot grade your plan, your bread or your photos: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference figures; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam, and this course does not award the CAP Boulanger.

## Brief

**Part A, the written phase (practice order, not an exam subject).** The exam's own rules for the written phase (time, documents allowed, points) are in [The CAP Boulanger Exam](../references/cap-exam.md); for this drill, give yourself 30 minutes.

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Commande d'entraînement 22-B**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Poids pâton | Fiche |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Baguettes de tradition (dont 1 épi) | 10 | 300 g | TR-01 |
> | Boules / tabatières | 3 / 3 | 400 g | TR-01 |
> | Petits pains, 3 formes | 20 | 60 g | TR-01 |
> | Pains complets moulés | 6 | 400 g | CO-01 |
> | Croissants / pains au chocolat / pains aux raisins | 8 / 8 / 8 | 60 g / 58 g / 50 g | CR-01 |
> | Pain brioché: 2 tresses à 2 branches; 8 animaux | 2 / 8 | 250 g / 60 g | PB-01 |
>
> Fournis en chambre froide (3 °C): pâte fermentée de tradition; crème pâtissière prête. Pertes 2 %; farine arrondie à la centaine de grammes supérieure. TR-01: farine 100, eau 68 (autolyse) + 2 (bassinage), sel 1,8, levure 0,6, pâte fermentée 15; TPV 23 °C.
> Relevés: farine 20 °C, fournil 22 °C, pâte fermentée environ 5 °C; facteur de friction TR-01 (4 facteurs, autolyse et pétrissage lent): 14.
> Un candidat. Début 6:30. Produits présentés à 13:30; poste propre à 13:45. Équipement comme la commande 22-A (lesson [22.2](../lessons/module-22/lesson-02.md)): un pétrin à spirale, four à soles 2 soles, four ventilé, armoire de pousse, cellule de refroidissement, chambre froide, laminoir.

In English: tradition bread in four shapes, six wholemeal tin loaves, a croissant lamination cut into three products and a pain brioché in braids and animals; one candidate from 6:30, products presented at 13:30.

**Part B, the home production day.** The home order of lesson [22.5](../lessons/module-22/lesson-05.md): PC-02 on 1 kg of flour (2 baguettes, 1 épi, a boule, a fendu, 6 rolls in three shapes), VI-01 on 500 g (3 baguettes viennoises), CR-01 on 500 g (4 croissants, 4 pains au chocolat, 4 pains aux raisins) with CP-01, and PL-01 on 250 g (a braid, 2 navettes, 2 hedgehogs). If you have an additive-free flour, you may replace PC-02 with TR-01 on 500 g of flour (lesson [09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)), shaped in the same four families.

**Part C, after the day.** Present and score everything, write your reports, fill in the readiness checklist with evidence, and write your plan for what you still need.

## You need

- Part A: calculator, timer, one [production sheet](../templates/production-sheet.md), one [organigramme](../templates/organigramme.md) (15-minute slots, 6:30-13:45), your recipe notebook with the course sheets TR-01 ([09.2](../lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)), CO-01 ([10.4](../lessons/module-10/lesson-04.md)), CR-01 ([11.2](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md)), PB-01 ([12.3](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md)).
- Part B: the equipment and ingredients listed in lesson [22.5](../lessons/module-22/lesson-05.md); a whole free day; an observer for at least half of it, with your checklist from lesson [22.1](../lessons/module-22/lesson-01.md); someone to taste.
- Part C: the [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md), the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md), the [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md), the [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md), the [readiness checklist](../templates/readiness-checklist.md), the [exam reference](../references/cap-exam.md) and your académie's page for individual candidates.
- Time: Part A 30 minutes plus 30 minutes of marking; Part B a full day plus the evening before; Part C about 2 hours.

## Steps

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses an oven at 250 °C with steam for hours, blades, scissors and possibly a stand mixer. Steam only into a metal tray preheated in the oven, never into glass or ceramic; dry oven gloves; blades moving away from your fingers; never reach into a running mixer; keep the floor dry; take a break when you planned one.

> [!CAUTION]
> Raw egg and allergens (gluten, milk, egg, soy, sulphites) are in Part B: separate bowl and brush for egg wash, hands washed after eggs, the cream cooled from 63 °C to 10 °C within 2 hours and kept cold, every product labelled with its allergens if anyone else eats it.

**Part A: written phase (30 minutes)**

1. Start the timer. Read and underline the order.
2. Complete the TR-01 technical sheet: totals, flour, every line including autolyse and bassinage water, check, water temperature, process with times and targets.
3. Write the batch flour for CO-01, CR-01 and PB-01 in one line each.
4. Name the critical path and the moves that make it fit the window (lesson 22.2); write the oven plan; draw the organigramme backwards from 13:30 with cleaning and presentation.
5. Stop at 30 minutes. Mark the sheet with the reference figures below and the organigramme with the six checks of lesson 22.2.

**Part B: the home production day**

6. Two weeks before: write your home organigramme and oven plan from your measured oven times; check it; arrange the observer and taster.
7. The evening before: pâte fermentée (or tradition pâte fermentée), equipment out, labels, plan on the wall.
8. Run the day. Write the real time beside every planned time and every temperature on the log. Decide and write when a dough is off target.
9. Write each non-conformity or malfunction on the report as it happens: facts and figures, what you did, what you propose.

**Part C: present, score, plan**

10. Present all products together after cooling, by family, counted and labelled; cut one baguette, the boule and one croissant.
11. Score every family with the quality rubric; collect the observer's checklist and the taster's comments.
12. Compare planned and real times; name the three biggest gaps and their causes; write two actions.
13. Fill in the readiness checklist: tick only with evidence (date, log, score, observer). For each unticked line, write an action, a place and a date.
14. Write one page, "What I still need": professional machines and exam-size batches, workplace experience, registration with your académie, general subjects or exemptions, clothing and protective equipment. Point to the exam reference and your académie's page for the rules; do not copy them.

## Deliverables

1. Part A: the TR-01 technical sheet, the three batch lines, the organigramme and oven plan for order 22-B, with your time and your marking.
2. Part B: your home organigramme and oven plan, written before the day, with real times written beside the planned ones.
3. The temperature log (every dough, the cream's two control points) and one bake log per product family.
4. At least two non-conformity or malfunction reports from the day.
5. The presented products: counts, weights, colour scores, cut sections, and a rubric score for every family.
6. The observer's checklist (or your own review of a recording) with the percentage of process lines ticked.
7. The readiness checklist with evidence for every tick and an action, place and date for every unticked line.
8. One page, "What I still need", pointing to the exam reference and your académie for the rules.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Written phase | Sheet with no calculation slip; organigramme complete in 30 minutes with critical path, oven plan, cleaning and presentation | Slips in the flour or water temperature; organigramme unfinished or drawn forwards with no oven plan |
| Planning the home day | Plan written in advance from measured oven times; every waiting dough has a named slow place; oven off when idle | Plan improvised on the day; trays waiting on the counter; oven running empty |
| Temperatures and food safety | Every dough within ± 1 °C or the decision written; cream 63 → 10 °C within 2 hours, logged; raw egg and allergens handled as planned | Temperatures not measured; cream cooled on the bench or not logged; egg wash shared with other tools |
| Technique | Pieces within ± 2 g; croissants 60 g ± 3 g; shapes regular in every family; lamination without smearing | Weights spread widely; families uneven; butter smeared or leaking |
| Hygiene, safety and cleaning | At least 80 % of the observer's process lines; clean station within 15 minutes of the last load | Clutter from mid-morning; hand washing or labels missed; long clean-up |
| Results | Every product present in its count; every family 14/20 or more on the rubric, or the cause and action written | Products missing; scores without causes |
| Reporting | Reports in the order facts → action → proposal, in professional vocabulary | Vague or blaming notes; problems not reported |
| What you still need | Checklist ticked only with evidence; every unticked line has an action, place and date; rules pointed to, not copied; no claim that the course awards the CAP | Lines ticked by hope; no plan for machines, workplace experience, registration or general subjects |
