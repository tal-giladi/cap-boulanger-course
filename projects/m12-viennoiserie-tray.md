# Project: Viennoiserie Tray

A bakery's viennoiserie bench works from two doughs every morning: one lamination cut into croissants, pains au chocolat and pains aux raisins, and one pâte levée shaped into braids and small pieces, with a crème pâtissière cooked and cooled in between. In Part A you prepare the paperwork for a Saturday order of that kind: the batches, the cream and raisins, the liquid temperature, the cooling and freezing plan, the oven plan and the allergen lines. In Part B you produce a complete home tray over two days: four croissants, four pains au chocolat and four pains aux raisins from one lamination, plus a braid, navettes and animals from a pâte levée. The Academy cannot grade your viennoiserie or your photos: compare your work honestly with the rubric and the reference answers; this self-assessment does not replace the official practical exam.

## Brief

> **Boulangerie Au Pain de la Halle — Commande de production, samedi (magasin 7:00)**
>
> | Produit | Quantité | Poids | Fiche |
> |---|---|---|---|
> | Croissants | 40 | pâton 60 g | pâte levée feuilletée |
> | Pains au chocolat | 40 | 58 g de pâte + 2 bâtons | pâte levée feuilletée |
> | Pains aux raisins | 40 | 50 g de pâte + 20 g de crème + 15 g de raisins réhydratés | pâte levée feuilletée + CP-01 |
> | Tresses à trois branches | 6 | 300 g | PB-01 |
> | Navettes | 20 | 50 g | PB-01 |
> | Hérissons | 20 | 60 g | PB-01 |
>
> Pâte levée feuilletée CR-01: farine T45 100 %, eau 26 %, lait 26 %, sucre 11 %, sel 2 %, levure fraîche 4 %, beurre 8 % (détrempe 177 %); beurrage 50 % du poids de farine (pâte feuilletée 227 %); pâte à tourer = pièces ÷ 0,90 (chutes 10 %) × 1,02 (pertes).
> PB-01: farine T45 100 %, lait 42 %, œufs 18 %, sucre 13 %, sel 2 %, levure fraîche 3,5 %, beurre 25 % (total 203,5 %); pertes de fabrication 2 %; TB 48 °C; pointage différé (30 min + nuit à +4 °C).
> CP-01 pour 1 000 g de lait: jaunes 160 g, sucre 220 g, amidon 90 g; rendement environ 1 400 g; prévoir 10 % de crème en plus. Raisins: 100 g secs = 125 g réhydratés.
> Farine arrondie à la centaine de grammes supérieure; lait de la crème arrondi à la centaine de grammes supérieure.
> Relevés vendredi 15:00: fournil 22 °C, farine 21 °C. Équipement: batteur, laminoir, cellule de refroidissement rapide, surgélateur, armoire de pousse programmable, un four ventilé (6 plaques).
> Le patron demande aussi 20 croissants crus surgelés pour dimanche, pris sur le même tourage.

In English: one lamination for 40 croissants, 40 pains au chocolat and 40 pains aux raisins (plus 20 croissants to freeze raw for Sunday), one PB-01 pâte levée for 6 braids, 20 navettes and 20 hedgehogs, the crème pâtissière and raisins for the pains aux raisins. Formulas, allowances, rounding rules, temperatures and equipment are given.

## You need

- Part A: calculator, two copies of the [production sheet template](../templates/production-sheet.md), the [organigramme template](../templates/organigramme.md), the [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md).
- Part B: the home equipment of lessons [12.1](../lessons/module-12/lesson-01.md) to [12.4](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md) (scales, probe thermometer, rolling pin with 4 mm and 3.5 mm guides, ruler, sharp knife or pastry wheel, scissors, heavy saucepan and two whisks, shallow tray, bowl of ice, cling film, pastry brush, two or three trays with baking paper, wire rack), chocolate batons, raisins, the [bake log](../templates/bake-log.md) and the [quality rubric](../templates/quality-rubric.md).
- Time for Part B: an evening (détrempe and pâte levée, both into the fridge) and a long day, about 5 hours of it hands-on, with proofs and rests in between.

> [!WARNING]
> Part B uses boiling milk and cream, a 180-200 °C oven, knives and scissors. Deep heavy pan, long whisk, a glove on the pan hand, pour away from you; dry oven gloves and stand back when you open the door; cut away from your fingers and put blades down flat. If you use a mixer, never put a hand or scraper in the bowl while it turns.

> [!CAUTION]
> Raw eggs and a high-risk cream: separate eggs over a separate bowl, wash your hands after the shells, use a clean whisk for the cooked cream, cool it from 63 °C to 10 °C in 2 hours or less, keep it in the fridge and discard it after 24 hours; egg wash fresh, cold, discarded after use. Allergens: wheat (gluten), milk, egg; soy and milk in most chocolate; sulphites if your raisins list them.

## Steps

### Part A — the Saturday order (about 90 minutes)

1. **Laminated dough.** Total dough for the three products (the 20 Sunday croissants included), ÷ 0.90 × 1.02; flour (rounded up); every détrempe ingredient and the beurrage; check the total. Number of chocolate batons.
2. **Crème pâtissière and raisins.** Cream needed with 10 %; the milk batch (rounded up) and its yolks, sugar and starch; dry raisins to weigh.
3. **PB-01.** Dough for braids, navettes and hedgehogs with 2 %; flour; every ingredient; strand weight for the braids.
4. **Liquid temperature** for PB-01 from the TB.
5. **Salt check:** salt per 100 g of a hedgehog that weighs 53 g after baking.
6. **Cream cooling plan:** container, depth, equipment, the two times you record, storage temperature and the latest time it may be used.
7. **Freezing plan for the 20 Sunday croissants:** when they are frozen, how they are packed and labelled, how and when they are thawed and proofed.
8. **Oven plan** for one 6-tray ventilated oven: which products go in which loads, at what temperature, in what order, so everything is cooled for 7:00.
9. **Allergen lines** for the croissants, pains au chocolat, pains aux raisins and PB-01 pieces.
10. **Fill in** one production sheet per dough and one organigramme for Friday afternoon to Saturday 7:00.

### Part B — your home tray (two days)

11. **Evening:** make the CR-01 détrempe for a 500 g-flour lamination (lesson [11.2](../lessons/module-11/lesson-02.md)) and **PB-01 on 250 g of flour** (or PL-01, lesson [12.3](../lessons/module-12/lesson-03.md)): mix to its target, 30 minutes with one fold, then flattened and filmed into the fridge. Soak nothing yet.
12. **Plan on paper** before you start the next morning: one timeline with the cream, the lamination, the three laminated products, the pâte levée shapes, both proofs and a one-tray oven. Which bakes first? Where does each tray proof in your kitchen (below about 27 °C)?
13. **Morning, first:** cook a small CP-01 (200 g milk) and cool it on ice with a cooling log; soak 30 g of raisins.
14. **Laminate** (lessons [11.3](../lessons/module-11/lesson-03.md) and [11.4](../lessons/module-11/lesson-04.md)) and divide the sheet: **4 croissants** of 60 g (lesson [11.5](../lessons/module-11/lesson-05.md)), **4 pains au chocolat** at 4 mm (lesson [12.1](../lessons/module-12/lesson-01.md)), **4 pains aux raisins** at 3.5 mm with about 75 g of cream (lesson [12.2](../lessons/module-12/lesson-02.md)). Optional: shape 2 more pains au chocolat and freeze them raw for the test of lesson [12.5](../lessons/module-12/lesson-05.md).
15. **Pâte levée:** divide the cold dough into a **three-strand braid of 240 g (3 × 80 g), 2 navettes of 50 g and 2 hedgehogs of 60 g**; shape (lesson [12.4](../lessons/module-12/lesson-04.md)); first egg wash.
16. **Proof, egg-wash and bake** in the order of your timeline; every tray below about 27 °C; small pieces and the braid on separate loads.
17. **Evaluate** after cooling: weigh every piece, cut one of each laminated product across the middle, score the croissant, the pain au chocolat, the pain aux raisins, the braid and the small pieces with the quality rubric. Discard the cream left over at 24 hours.
18. **Write three lines:** what matched your timeline, what did not, the one change for next time.

## Deliverables

- Part A: the calculations for steps 1-9, two production sheets, one organigramme, the cooling plan, the freezing plan and the oven plan.
- Part B: the timeline, the cream cooling log, the temperature log of both doughs, the bake log with all times and weights, the salt check of one pâte levée piece, five rubric scores and your three lines.
- Optional: a photo of the whole tray and of a cut section of each laminated product, kept with your log for your own comparison.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Laminated dough | All products (and the Sunday croissants) counted; ÷ 0.90 × 1.02; flour from the laminated total (227 %); beurrage from the flour | A product forgotten; flour from the détrempe total only; beurrage from the dough weight |
| Cream and raisins | Cream with 10 %; milk batch from the yield; raisins converted back to dry weight | Milk weight taken as cream weight; soaked weight bought as dry |
| PB-01 and liquids | Dough with 2 %; flour rounded up; liquids = TB − fournil − flour | Friction taken out of a printed TB again |
| Food-safety plan | Shallow filmed tray, blast chiller or ice; 63 and 10 °C times recorded within 2 h; 0-3 °C; 24 h | Deep bowl; one temperature only; cream kept "until it runs out" |
| Freezing plan | Frozen straight after shaping, bagged and labelled; thawed in the cold; never refrozen; no bread in the freezer | Frozen after proofing; thawed on the bench; no label |
| Home laminated products | Pains au chocolat with covered batons and seam underneath; pains aux raisins round with tails tucked and golden bases; open, separate layers in each cut section | Chocolate showing; spirals unrolled; pale wet bases; greasy grey band |
| Home pâte levée | Braid symmetrical, braided from the middle, no splits; small pieces within ± 2 g and identical | Braid uneven; pieces of different sizes |
| Proof, bake and control | Every tray proofed below about 27 °C; pieces golden including crossings and bases; everything weighed; rubrics completed | Butter on the tray; pale or burnt pieces; weights or rubric missing |
| Safety and hygiene | Boiling cream, oven and blades handled as in the warnings; egg wash and cream discarded on time; allergens listed | Steps skipped |
