# Project: Hygiene Audit

This project brings Module 17 together. You audit a bakery's hygiene file and a morning of its work on paper, the way an internal audit or an inspection would, then audit your own kitchen, build a small hygiene file (a home "PMS") for your fournil, and finish with an action plan and a non-conformity report. The aim is the professional habit behind competencies C2.5 and C2.6: see the hazard, name the rule, act, record.

## Brief

You have just been hired as ouvrier boulanger at Boulangerie Saint-Roch. The owner leaves you this note:

> **Note du patron — audit hygiène avant le contrôle**
>
> La DDPP peut passer à tout moment. Fais le tour comme un inspecteur :
> 1. Lis les documents et les observations ci-dessous et trouve toutes les non-conformités.
> 2. Pour chacune : la règle, l'action immédiate, la prévention, la priorité.
> 3. Fais la même chose chez toi, dans ta cuisine, pour t'entraîner.
> 4. Prépare-moi un dossier propre : plan de nettoyage, relevés de températures, tableau des allergènes, une analyse HACCP, un contrôle à réception, l'évaluation des risques.
> 5. Termine par un plan d'actions et une fiche de non-conformité pour le problème le plus grave.

In English: the inspectors can come at any time. (1) Read the documents and observations and find every non-conformity. (2) For each: the rule, the immediate action, the prevention, the priority. (3) Do the same in your own kitchen. (4) Build a clean file: cleaning plan, temperature logs, allergen table, one HACCP analysis, one reception check, the risk assessment. (5) Finish with an action plan and a non-conformity report for the most serious problem.

### Observations during the morning

1. The tourier wears a watch and a wedding ring while laminating.
2. A sales assistant takes payment, then picks up two croissants with her fingers.
3. A box of eggs is cracked on the finishing bench next to cooled crème pâtissière.
4. Flour sacks are stacked on the floor against the wall of the dry store; one is torn with dust trails.
5. An opened 3 kg pack of ham in the cold room has no date.
6. The bin in the fournil has a lift-up lid; the outdoor bin stands by the back door of the fournil.
7. A bottle of descaler has been decanted into an empty cream bottle under the plonge.
8. The floor by the oven is wet and floury; the apprentice sweeps it with a broom at the end of the shift.
9. The pastry worker has a bandaged burn on her hand and is piping cream without a glove.
10. Frozen pains au chocolat are thawing on a rack in the fournil at 24 °C, overnight.
11. Thawed croissants are sold with no information.
12. 25 kg sacks are lifted from the floor into the open spiral mixer by one person.

## You need

- The documents and observations above.
- Templates: [delivery check](../templates/delivery-check.md), [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md), [temperature log](../templates/temperature-log.md) (use its "Measured" table idea for a cold-unit log, or the log layout of lesson 17.8).
- Your kitchen, fridge, freezer and dry store; probe thermometer; your technical sheets for 6 products; labels of your bought ingredients.
- Time: about 1 hour 30 for Part A, 45 minutes for Part B, about 4 hours spread over a week for Part C (the temperature log runs 7 days), 30 minutes for Part D.

> [!CAUTION]
> In Part B and C you handle cleaning products: read the labels, wear gloves, use cold water for bleach, never mix products (bleach with an acid releases a toxic gas). Inspect your oven and mixer only when the oven is cold and the mixer unplugged.

## Steps

### Part A — the paper audit (about 1 h 30)

1. For each document and each observation, decide whether there is a non-conformity. Write it as one row:

| No. | What I found (facts) | Rule broken | Lesson | Immediate action | Prevention | Priority (1 high - 3 low) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

2. Group your rows by the 5M (people, equipment, raw materials, methods, surroundings) and count them.
3. Choose the three most serious and explain why in one sentence each (risk to the consumer first, then to staff).
4. Only then open the reference audit and mark your own.

### Part B — your own kitchen (about 45 minutes)

5. Audit your kitchen with this checklist; for each "no", write the action and the date:
   - Hand-washing point with soap and single-use paper; no jewellery when baking; hair tied and covered.
   - Phone kept out of the work area.
   - Fridge middle shelf at +4 °C or below (probed); freezer at −18 °C.
   - Ready-to-eat above raw; nothing perishable in the door; every opened product dated.
   - Flour and seeds in closed containers, off the floor; older stock in front.
   - Cleaning products in their own containers, in a separate cupboard; bleach never with acids.
   - Bench and tools cleaned, then disinfected with the right dose and contact time.
   - Bin with a lid, emptied after a bake; no crumbs or flour left overnight (pests).
   - Oven gloves dry and within reach; tray landing spot next to the oven; floor dry.
   - Heaviest load lifted from waist height; mixer unplugged before cleaning.

### Part C — your home hygiene file (about 4 hours over a week)

6. **Cleaning and disinfection plan** for your fournil (lesson [17.4](../lessons/module-17/lesson-04.md)), with doses in mL.
7. **7-day temperature log** for your fridge and freezer and **one stock sheet** (lesson [17.8](../lessons/module-17/lesson-08.md)).
8. **Allergen table** for six products, 14 allergens plus traces (lesson [17.6](../lessons/module-17/lesson-06.md)).
9. **One HACCP table** for a sensitive product you make (crème pâtissière, a sandwich or a quiche): flow diagram, hazards, CCPs with limits, monitoring, corrective action, record (lesson [17.5](../lessons/module-17/lesson-05.md)).
10. **One reception check**: treat your next grocery shopping as a delivery and fill the [delivery check](../templates/delivery-check.md) (lesson [17.7](../lessons/module-17/lesson-07.md)).
11. **Risk assessment** of your workstation with scores and three actions done (lesson [17.9](../lessons/module-17/lesson-09.md)).

### Part D — action plan and report (30 minutes)

12. From Parts A and B, write an action plan: the five highest priorities, each with what, who, by when, and how you will check it was done.
13. Write one [non-conformity report](../templates/non-conformity-report.md) for the most serious finding of Part A: facts and measurements, immediate action, probable cause, suggested correction and prevention.

## Deliverables

- Part A: the audit table (at least 14 findings), grouped by 5M, with your top three justified, marked against the reference.
- Part B: the completed kitchen checklist with actions and dates.
- Part C: the hygiene file: cleaning plan, 7-day log and stock sheet, allergen table, one HACCP table, one delivery check, one risk assessment.
- Part D: the action plan and one non-conformity report.

## Self-evaluation rubric

| Criterion | Meets standard | Needs work |
|---|---|---|
| Finding non-conformities (Part A) | 14 or more of the 17 found; each with the rule and the lesson | fewer than 10; rules missing or vague |
| Priorities | consumer safety risks (cooling, delivery, allergens, chemicals) ranked first, with reasons | priorities by appearance (tidiness) rather than risk |
| Actions | immediate action and prevention distinct, concrete, with a person | "be careful", "pay attention" |
| Temperatures and times | numbers match the course: 0 to +4 °C (creams 0 to +3 °C), −18 °C, +7 °C / −15 °C limits, 63 → 10 °C in 2 h, 24 h for crème pâtissière, 3 days after opening | numbers missing or wrong |
| Hygiene file (Part C) | all six documents present, filled with real measurements, no blanks | documents missing; logs copied rather than measured |
| Allergens | 14 allergens checked against real labels; traces column filled | only the obvious allergens; labels not read |
| Safety | chemicals used one at a time with gloves; oven and mixer inspected safely | products mixed; inspection with the oven hot or the mixer plugged in |
| Report (Part D) | facts and measurements first; action; probable cause; prevention; no blame | impressions or blame; no prevention |

The Academy cannot see your kitchen or check your logs: compare your work honestly with this rubric and the reference audit. This self-assessment helps you learn; it does not replace the official exam, where hygiene and safety are assessed as described in [The CAP Boulanger Exam](../references/cap-exam.md).
